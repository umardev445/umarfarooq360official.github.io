Study in Turkey: universities, scholarships and costs

Build a study plan for Türkiye that connects the university offer, funding decision and arrival paperwork. Start with your degree level, teaching language and the cost you can meet without an unconfirmed scholarship.

The short answer

Choose a recognised programme, check its international admission route, calculate the cost after confirmed funding, then arrange the correct entry and residence documents. There is no single application covering every Turkish university.

Next step: Use the route table to identify your first decision. University admission and a scholarship application can run on different calendars.

Compare public and foundation universities

Keep a shortlist of actual programmes rather than university names alone. Two departments in the same institution can differ in language, professional recognition, fees and document deadlines. Record the campus too: a city label does not tell you the commute.

Start with METU and Istanbul University for the public-university route, and Sabancı, Koç and Bilkent for foundation-university admission and funding comparisons. These five are researched starting points, not an exhaustive list or a claim about the best university for every subject.

Separate three scholarship decisions

Türkiye Bursları, the joint IsDB–YTB route and a university tuition award are separate funding decisions. A university discount should not be entered into the budget as a cash stipend. Read the award letter for duration, accommodation, language preparation and payments still due.

The reviewed 2026 IsDB–YTB application period ended on 20 February 2026. This hub does not announce a 2027 reopening. Prepare documents while waiting for a named official call, and keep a self-funded alternative only if its cost is realistic.

Build the cost before choosing a city

Add tuition, occupied months of accommodation, everyday spending and arrival expenses. Keep USD and TRY in separate columns until you choose and date an exchange rate. A dorm bill for the teaching year is not automatically a twelve-month housing quote.

Istanbul versus Ankara is useful only when the room type, commute, bills and months match. Compare the university-specific housing examples in our living-cost guide; they are not city-wide average rents.

Worked example

Fictional planning case: an applicant receives two offers. Offer A has lower tuition but no confirmed room; Offer B has a tuition waiver and an actual dorm quote. Compare the remaining annual cost and the cash due before arrival, rather than choosing the larger scholarship percentage.

Follow the application and arrival sequence

Make one calendar with separate academic, funding and legal tasks. Keep conditional offer requirements visible until the university confirms they have been satisfied.

  1. 1Choose degree level, programme language, campus and recognition requirements.
  2. 2Match your school qualification or prior degree to the institution’s accepted route.
  3. 3Submit the university and relevant scholarship applications separately.
  4. 4Confirm the written offer, net tuition, refundable payments and housing.
  5. 5Use the competent Turkish mission’s entry instructions for your nationality.
  6. 6After enrolment, follow the university and migration authority’s residence process.

Erasmus+ exchange is an institution-led route

If you are already studying at a university, ask its international office whether a relevant Turkish partner and funded mobility call exist. The Turkish National Agency describes higher-education mobility through participating institutions. An exchange place is different from admission to a complete Turkish degree.

Before accepting a mobility offer, agree course recognition, study dates and funding with the sending institution. Ask which costs the grant actually meets and who arranges housing. Do not assume a university on your preferred city list has an active agreement for your department.

Official reference: Turkish National Agency: higher-education learning mobility

Work and plans after graduation

A student residence permit is not an unrestricted work permission. The labour ministry’s current criteria distinguish associate/bachelor’s students from graduate students; the first group can be considered for part-time work after completing the first year, through the work-permit process.

Do not use hoped-for earnings to close a compulsory tuition or visa-funding gap. Before graduation, ask the university and migration authority about your actual residence position. A possible residence route and permission to work are separate questions.

Official reference: Ministry of Labour: foreign-student criteria

Frequently asked questions

Can I study in Turkey in English?+

Yes, English-taught routes exist, but check the exact programme and its preparation or exemption rules. An English-language university website does not prove that every degree is taught in English.

Is study in Turkey free for international students?+

Admission itself does not make study free. Tuition and everyday costs remain unless your actual scholarship or exemption covers them. Compare the net offer and excluded costs.

Can Pakistani students apply to Turkish universities?+

Eligible Pakistani applicants can use the institution’s international admission route. University entry, scholarship eligibility and the consular visa process each require separate checks.

Are 2027 scholarship applications open?+

This guide does not confirm an open 2027 Türkiye Bursları or joint IsDB–YTB call. Use the named official announcement rather than extending a previous year’s deadline.
