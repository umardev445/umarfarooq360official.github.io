Choose a degree before choosing a funding headline

Start with the exact bachelor's, single-cycle, master's or doctoral programme. Record the teaching language, academic prerequisites, intake, application portal and whether admission is limited by a test or ranking. For a bachelor's route, a foreign school qualification normally needs to represent at least 12 years of education; university-specific rules explain how shorter school systems are assessed.

For a master's application, compare your previous degree, subjects, credits and grades with the programme requirements. A university can reject an academically mismatched application even when the applicant qualifies for a general scholarship category.

  • Exact programme and campus
  • Degree level and prior qualification
  • Teaching language
  • Admission call and deadline
  • Application fee and document format
  • Separate funding deadline

English requirements are programme-specific

Italy does not have one IELTS score for every university. English-taught programmes commonly ask for a CEFR level such as B1 or B2 and publish their own accepted certificates, minimum scores and deadlines. Some institutions may accept a completed qualification taught entirely in English when the official documents clearly state the language of instruction.

Treat a Medium of Instruction letter as evidence to be checked, not a universal IELTS waiver. Verify the exact course notice, scholarship condition and consular checklist. An English-taught degree can also carry a later Italian-language learning requirement at some institutions.

Understand tuition before estimating the scholarship gap

Public-university tuition can depend on the programme, study load, citizenship or residence category and the family's economic documentation. For 2026–2027, the University of Padua describes general degree costs from zero to about €3,000 a year. Politecnico di Milano shows a standard annual range from €157.04 up to €3,943.04, while reserved non-EU master's students with a foreign qualification may be charged the maximum when no qualifying scholarship applies.

The University of Bologna uses a fixed €157.04 component plus a variable programme and economic component. These examples are planning references for a named academic year. Your final amount comes from the university account after your documents are assessed.

Follow the admission, Universitaly and visa sequence

Apply first through the university's stated admission system. After a positive evaluation, non-EU applicants living abroad generally complete the required pre-enrolment through Universitaly. The university validates the request so it can support the study-visa process with the relevant Italian diplomatic mission.

University validation does not guarantee a visa. Follow the embassy or consulate serving your place of residence for financial, accommodation, insurance and document requirements. Keep university admission, scholarship, Universitaly and visa tasks as four separate rows in your tracker.

A practical Italy shortlist

For every programme, create one record containing the official degree page, admission call, tuition page, language rule, scholarship owner and current deadline. Add a personal budget showing tuition after any waiver, housing, food, transport, insurance, visa-related costs and money needed before the first benefit payment.

Frequently asked questions

Can international students study in Italy with a scholarship?+

Yes. Possible routes include MAECI, regional DSU benefits, university merit awards, fee waivers and research funding. Eligibility, coverage and application ownership differ.

Can I study in Italy without IELTS?+

Possibly, when the exact programme accepts another certificate or documented prior education in English. There is no country-wide IELTS waiver, so verify the programme's accepted evidence.

Are Italian public universities free?+

Not automatically. Fees can be reduced according to income, status or a waiver, but each university publishes its own calculation, deadlines and mandatory charges.

Is Universitaly the university application portal?+

Usually it is a later pre-enrolment step for eligible international applicants. You first follow the university's own admission instructions unless that institution explicitly states another route.

Does a scholarship guarantee an Italian student visa?+

No. Admission, funding and the visa are separate decisions. The competent Italian diplomatic mission makes the visa decision using its current requirements.
