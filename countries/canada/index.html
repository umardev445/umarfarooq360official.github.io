Where Canadian funding is found

Start at the department and graduate faculty, not only the central scholarship page. Funding packages can combine teaching assistantships, research assistantships, entrance awards and supervisor support.

At undergraduate level, large entrance awards are highly selective and usually require early admission applications or school nomination.

  • Compare gross funding with tuition and city costs
  • Ask whether funding is guaranteed each year
  • Check supervisor contact expectations
  • Review province-specific study requirements
  • Confirm award eligibility for international status

Separate undergraduate awards from graduate funding packages

Major undergraduate awards can require nomination before the normal application closes. For the University of Toronto's 2027 Lester B. Pearson cycle, the official page lists a school-nomination deadline, an admission deadline and a later scholarship-application deadline. Missing the first step cannot be repaired by submitting only the scholarship form.

Research master's and doctoral funding is often assembled by the university or department from an entrance award, supervisor grant and teaching or research work. Compare the written package after tuition and mandatory fees, and ask whether each component is guaranteed or conditional.

Questions to ask before accepting Canadian funding

Read the offer and programme handbook together. A headline annual amount may include employment that has separate hours, duties and renewal rules.

  • Is the quoted amount before or after tuition?
  • How many years are guaranteed?
  • Are teaching or research hours included?
  • Does funding continue during fieldwork or leave?
  • Which health-insurance and student fees remain?
  • Is a supervisor commitment required before admission?

Pearson 2027: nominate, apply, then complete the award

For September 2027 entry, the University of Toronto's published schedule is 9 October 2026 for a school's nomination, 16 October for undergraduate admission and 6 November for the scholarship application. These dates are three distinct actions; the university issues the private scholarship form after school nomination.

The award describes tuition, books, incidental fees and full residence support for four years. Confirm programme admission conditions and plan for any expenses not included in this published benefit list. Our dedicated Pearson guide breaks down the complete nomination sequence.

Graduate funding: request a written breakdown

Ask the programme or department whether the headline amount comes from a guaranteed stipend, a teaching assistantship, a supervisor's project or a competitive award. Confirm the term covered and what remains after tuition and compulsory charges.

Estimate living expenses for the actual city, then ask whether a research supervisor must agree to support the application before admission. Undergraduate nomination advice does not transfer to this route.

Pearson's remaining dates are not a new nomination window

For the named September 2027 Pearson cycle, school nomination ended on 9 October 2026. The remaining 16 October admission and 6 November scholarship tasks apply to already nominated students. If you missed nomination, compare other undergraduate routes instead of treating the later date as a fresh application opening.

Frequently asked questions

Are Canadian scholarships automatically considered with admission?+

Some are, but many are not. Major awards may require a separate form, early admission or school nomination. Read the official award page and the department's funding instructions.

Does a funded PhD offer cover the full cost of living in Canada?+

Not necessarily. Compare the net amount after tuition and fees with housing, food, transport and insurance in the specific city, and check whether part of the package requires paid work.

Do Canadian scholarships use one national application system?+

No. University awards, research funding and external scholarships can have different application routes. Check the specific institution and award owner.

Can an undergraduate scholarship require school nomination?+

Yes. Some major awards, including the University of Toronto Pearson route, use a school nomination before later admission and scholarship steps. Follow the current official sequence.

How should I evaluate a graduate funding offer in Canada?+

Ask for the guaranteed amount, duration, tuition and compulsory fees, assistantship duties, renewal conditions and what remains after charges. Then compare the net amount with costs in the actual city.
