Tuition-free is not cost-free

Many public institutions charge low or no tuition for selected programmes, yet students still need to plan for semester contributions, housing, insurance, travel and visa proof of funds. Some states or programmes charge tuition to non-EU students.

DAAD's database is the best starting point for its current programmes. EPOS is aimed at applicants from eligible countries and generally expects relevant professional experience.

  • Check the exact programme language
  • Use the official DAAD scholarship database
  • Confirm APS or document-verification rules where relevant
  • Compare city-level living costs
  • Review work-experience requirements carefully

How to research a German programme

Check the university's programme page for language, subject prerequisites, application route and semester dates. Some applications go directly to the university, while others use uni-assist or another stated process. Follow the exact programme instructions.

For DAAD EPOS, applicants normally apply to an eligible postgraduate course and must meet course-specific deadlines. The programme is aimed at applicants from eligible developing and newly industrialised countries and generally expects at least two years of relevant professional experience.

Plan documents and visa evidence

Allow time for certified copies, translations and any country-specific academic verification. A scholarship decision does not remove the need to meet university admission and German immigration requirements.

Build a Germany application sequence

First confirm that your previous degree meets the programme's academic prerequisites. Next identify whether admission uses the university, uni-assist or another route. Then check whether the scholarship application is combined or separate.

For research degrees, read the department and supervisor process before sending a proposal. Use official DAAD and university pages for the current intake rather than copying an older checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Is studying in Germany free for every international student?+

No. Tuition and fees depend on the state, institution and programme, and living costs still apply. Confirm the current fee schedule for the exact course.

Where do I apply for DAAD EPOS?+

Applications are normally submitted to the selected eligible postgraduate course according to its instructions, not through one universal DAAD application. Check the current DAAD listing and course deadline.

Is studying in Germany completely free for international students?+

Not always. Tuition policy varies by institution and programme, and students still need to plan semester contributions, insurance, housing, food, travel and other costs.

Does every DAAD scholarship require work experience?+

No. Requirements differ by programme. DAAD EPOS generally targets development-related postgraduate courses and relevant professional experience, but other awards use different rules.
