Plan two applications, not one

For many UK awards, scholarship selection and university admission are connected but not identical. Chevening asks applicants to identify eligible courses, while Commonwealth routes can involve a nominating body. Gates Cambridge is considered through the Cambridge graduate application.

Create one calendar for scholarship deadlines and another for course admission, references, English tests and document uploads.

  • Check course eligibility for the award
  • Understand conditional and unconditional offers
  • Confirm reference submission rules
  • Budget visa and immigration health costs if not covered
  • Track deposit deadlines while awaiting funding decisions

Compare the amount you will actually receive

Record tuition coverage, stipend, travel, visa or arrival support, dependants, deposits and any fee cap. For partial university awards, subtract the discount from total tuition and add living costs for the actual city.

Confirm whether the scholarship requires a return-home commitment, leadership evidence, work experience or an unconditional offer by a later date.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply to a UK scholarship before university admission?+

It depends on the award. Chevening has a separate scholarship process and a later offer requirement, while many university awards require an admission application first.

Does a UK scholarship automatically waive IELTS?+

No. Scholarship and university language rules are separate. Check the exact course and accepted evidence for the relevant intake.
