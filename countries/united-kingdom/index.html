Plan two applications, not one

For many UK awards, scholarship selection and university admission are connected but not identical. Chevening asks applicants to identify eligible courses, while Commonwealth routes can involve a nominating body. Gates Cambridge is considered through the Cambridge graduate application.

Create one calendar for scholarship deadlines and another for course admission, references, English tests and document uploads.

  • Check course eligibility for the award
  • Understand conditional and unconditional offers
  • Confirm reference submission rules
  • Budget visa and immigration health costs if not covered
  • Track deposit deadlines while awaiting funding decisions

Compare the amount you will actually receive

Record tuition coverage, stipend, travel, visa or arrival support, dependants, deposits and any fee cap. For partial university awards, subtract the discount from total tuition and add living costs for the actual city.

Confirm whether the scholarship requires a return-home commitment, leadership evidence, work experience or an unconditional offer by a later date.

Choose a UK funding route for your degree level

A country shortlist becomes useful when it distinguishes the award, university place and remaining personal costs. Start with your intended degree, field and citizenship, then compare only routes that could accept that profile. A funding brand is not a guarantee that every course or applicant is eligible.

Do not confuse a fee discount with an affordable offer

Ask for the total course fee and whether an award reduces all tuition, a capped amount or selected charges. Then add accommodation, food, transport, insurance or other required payments, travel and initial cash. A partial award can be useful while still leaving a large funding gap.

For a fictional comparison, a course with a 12,000-unit fee and a 3,000-unit discount leaves 9,000 before living costs. Another course with a lower fee may be more affordable even if its advertised discount is smaller. These invented figures teach the comparison and are not UK price estimates.

Record when each payment is due. An eventual stipend cannot pay a deposit that must be transferred before the award decision. Ask about refund conditions before accepting a financial commitment.

Make a programme-specific application worksheet

Create one row per course, even when several courses belong to the same university. Include the programme title, mode, intake, eligibility, language evidence, admission portal, funding application and deadlines. Save the official page used for every important condition.

Separate the tasks you control from the decisions you are waiting for. An application submitted is not an offer, and an offer is not a scholarship. Conditional admission may still require a qualification or language result before enrolment.

Example: an applicant comparing two funding routes

A fictional graduate has relevant work experience but needs substantial support for living costs. One option offers a tuition reduction attached to admission; another requires a separate leadership-focused scholarship application. The graduate first checks mandatory eligibility, then compares net cost and what must be submitted to each decision owner.

If the larger award is already closed, the sensible next step is not to label it open because the course still accepts admission. The applicant can assess another viable route or prepare for a later officially announced cycle. Keep any expected future opening separate from a confirmed call.

Use the dedicated scholarship guides for detailed conditions, and the writing guides to prepare evidence that belongs to your own experience.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for funding before receiving an offer?+

It depends on the award. Check the order of scholarship and university applications and any later offer requirement.

Does the scholarship waive course language requirements?+

Do not assume so. The university and scholarship can have different evidence requirements.

Should I pay a deposit while waiting for a scholarship?+

Read the payment and refund conditions and evaluate your confirmed budget. A pending award is not money already available.
