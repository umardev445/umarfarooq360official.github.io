uni-assist application: documents, fees and the right submission route

Find out what your chosen German university actually asks uni-assist to do, then prepare the right documents and keep proof of submission.

Updated 20 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

uni-assist evaluates international educational qualifications for participating universities. Your course may use a standard application, a university-specific VPD or a different direct route. The university’s instructions decide which one applies.

Next step: Open the admission instructions for your exact course, qualification country and intended semester before creating or paying for an application.

First decide which route applies

Begin with the university’s course page, not a search for the cheapest portal. Record the degree, semester, qualification country and applicant category. A university can use different procedures for different programmes, so an instruction from another department is not enough.

A standard uni-assist application and a VPD request are different workflows. In the VPD procedure, you still apply directly to the university with the assessment. For a direct university route, do not buy an unnecessary uni-assist assessment just because another applicant needed one.

Prepare educational evidence before uploading

Make an inventory of your actual qualification history: school leaving certificate, university study, degree certificate when available, transcript and the grading information supplied by the issuing institution. Compare it with the country instructions and course requirements. Do not assume one generic document checklist covers every applicant.

Use clear scans that include all pages and any relevant reverse side. Keep the original-language document with the required translation rather than replacing it with an English summary you wrote yourself. Certification, translation and additional country-specific evidence must follow the official instructions for your case.

Calculate the handling fee by course and semester

The checked official fee schedule charges €75 for the first chosen course and €30 for each additional chosen course in the same semester. The fee covers evaluation, not admission or tuition. A new semester can create a new charge.

Count courses, not just university names: two courses at one institution are still two choices. Before paying, check whether a university has a specific arrangement and whether your selected procedure is correct. Never treat a fee receipt as confirmation that the academic requirements have been met.

Submit deliberately: account, choices, documents and payment

Use your own email address and retain control of the account. Choose the correct semester and course or VPD option, then compare the generated application summary with the university instruction. Fix a wrong degree type or target university before it becomes a processing problem.

Read each portal status after uploading and payment. A document in an upload area, a saved draft and a submitted application are different things. Save the final confirmation and fee record together so you can identify the application if you need to contact support.

Work backwards from the deadline

Write down the deadline shown for your exact programme and category, including any time zone. Then set earlier personal dates for obtaining documents, translations and payment. Do not use 15 July or 15 January as a universal promise: university and programme windows differ.

For VPD requests, uni-assist describes a usual four-to-six-week processing period and recommends starting ideally at least eight weeks before the university deadline. That is a planning buffer, not guaranteed delivery. Incomplete documents or follow-up questions can extend the process.

Read the result and finish the university application

Check what the evaluation actually says and whether another action is needed. Keep the assessment, portal correspondence and any university reference number together. If something appears incorrect, write a specific question with the relevant qualification and document page.

A positive educational evaluation is not an admission offer. Course selection, language evidence, aptitude assessment and funding may remain the university’s responsibility. The VPD route particularly needs a second submission; put that task in your calendar before waiting for the result.

Avoid unnecessary documents and unsafe shortcuts

Use the required list instead of uploading a large unrelated portfolio. For example, the TUM VPD instructions explain that items such as motivation letters and language certificates are not needed for that assessment, although the university application can require them separately. This illustrates why assessment and admission files should be treated as related but distinct.

Keep passwords private and use official support channels. uni-assist’s official information warns that it does not cooperate with commercial agencies to place applicants at German universities. No agency can turn an evaluation fee into guaranteed admission.

Frequently asked questions

Is uni-assist the university?+

No. It evaluates qualifications for participating institutions. The university makes its admission decision under its own programme rules.

How much do two choices cost?+

Under the checked standard schedule, two chosen courses in the same semester cost €105: €75 for the first and €30 for the additional course. Check any institution-specific arrangement before paying.

Is uploading documents enough?+

No. Follow the portal’s payment and submission steps and retain confirmation. A saved draft or uploaded file is not automatically a completed application.

Does every German university require uni-assist?+

No. The exact university, programme and qualification instructions determine whether you use a standard application, VPD or direct route.

Can uni-assist guarantee admission?+

No. Evaluation does not replace university selection, language checks, programme requirements or funding decisions.
