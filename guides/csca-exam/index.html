CSCA exam: requirements, subjects and registration

Preparing an undergraduate application to China? Confirm your programme’s CSCA requirements before booking an examination or choosing subjects.

Updated 22 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

CSCA is the China Scholastic Competency Assessment for undergraduate academic readiness. The target university and programme determine how its requirements apply to your application and which subject results you need.

Next step: Read the current university undergraduate guide and record its required CSCA subjects, test language and result-submission deadline.

What CSCA does, and which applicants should investigate it

Shanghai’s official guide describes CSCA as an assessment of language proficiency and academic readiness for undergraduate study. It identifies a requirement in undergraduate applications at universities offering Chinese Government Scholarships. Your university’s current instructions remain essential for the application you are actually making.

Do not turn that statement into a requirement for every master’s, doctoral, short-course or exchange applicant. Check degree level first. Then confirm whether your chosen undergraduate programme requires a result at submission, later in assessment or under a specified scholarship route.

Choose the subject combination from the programme rule

Read the official CSCA syllabus and the university’s subject table together. Mathematics, physics, chemistry and Chinese-related assessment can play different roles across programmes. Do not choose subjects simply because another applicant to China selected them.

The teaching language and the examination language are separate details to confirm. Keep a screenshot or copy of the university instruction with its intake year, especially if you are comparing engineering, science and non-science degrees.

Registration and test arrangements

Start from csca.cn and use its official registration route. Check the currently offered dates, testing arrangements, identification rules and payment instructions before paying. Third-party preparation packages are not the official examination registration.

If the examination is offered remotely for your sitting, read the technical and room requirements in full. Test your device and connection using the organiser’s instructions. If a centre-based option applies, check the venue and arrival time. Keep the confirmation for the exact sitting you booked.

A preparation plan based on evidence

Download the current official subject outline and available examples. Compare it with your school topics to identify gaps rather than buying a broad course immediately. Divide your study time between learning missing material and practising under the stated time conditions.

Keep a short error log by subject: conceptual mistake, algebra or calculation error, misunderstanding the instruction, or time management. Revise the weakest category first. Any study schedule here is an editorial suggestion, not an official score guarantee.

Results, university selection and scholarships

A result is one part of the application alongside school records and other required material. Do not assume a score guarantees admission or government funding. The university’s selection method and scholarship competition remain separate decisions.

Follow the university’s method for submitting or verifying the score. If the result date appears later than the document deadline, ask the admissions office whether a later upload is permitted. An exam booking receipt should not be assumed to replace an actual result.

Avoid three common mistakes

First, do not treat an old syllabus, fee or timetable as the current sitting. Second, do not use another university’s subject combination without checking your programme. Third, do not repeat unverified claims about pass rates or universal minimum scores.

Use the latest official notices if a test arrangement changes. Keep the source, publication year and date you checked it in your application tracker. That record is useful when a university or examination office asks which instructions you followed.

Frequently asked questions

Is CSCA the same as HSK?+

No. Do not treat an academic-readiness assessment and a Chinese-language certificate as interchangeable. Follow the programme’s requirements for each.

Do all postgraduate applicants need CSCA?+

This guide concerns undergraduate applicability. Check postgraduate requirements directly rather than applying an undergraduate rule to a master’s or doctorate.

Does a CSCA score guarantee a scholarship?+

No. Admission and scholarship selection use the relevant institution’s rules and other application evidence.
