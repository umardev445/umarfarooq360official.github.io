Study in Germany: admission requirements for international students

German admission starts with the exact degree programme, recognition of your previous qualification and the correct application route. A country-level checklist cannot replace the university's current programme rules.

Updated 21 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Your qualification, intended degree and programme rules determine German university admission. Check direct-entry eligibility first, then the application route, subject and language requirements. Admission, scholarship selection and the visa remain separate decisions.

Next step: Use your chosen degree page and the official qualification-assessment guidance. Identify whether you need direct entry, preparatory study or a university-specific review.

Check whether you qualify for direct entry

The DAAD admission database gives orientation based on educational background and is prepared with uni-assist, but the higher education institution makes the final admission decision. Save the result as a research note, not as an admission certificate.

If a school-leaving qualification is not sufficient for direct undergraduate entry, a Studienkolleg and the relevant assessment examination may be required. Master's applicants must separately match the first degree, subject content, credits and grade rules published by the programme.

Direct application, uni-assist and VPD are different routes

Some institutions receive applications directly. Others ask uni-assist to evaluate and forward an application. A VPD route adds another step: uni-assist evaluates the documents, then the applicant submits the VPD and university application directly before the university deadline.

A VPD is issued for the chosen university and normally takes four to six weeks after a complete submission. Request it early; receiving the VPD does not submit the university application for you.

Prepare documents without losing context

Common categories include the degree or school certificate, complete transcript, the institution's grading system, language evidence and programme-specific material. Upload complete documents rather than selected pages.

Where a certificate is not issued in German or English, uni-assist generally asks for the original-language document plus a certified translation into German or English. Check country-specific notes and the university's instructions before ordering copies.

Keep admission, funding and visa as separate decisions

An academic offer does not automatically provide a scholarship or satisfy the visa proof-of-funds requirement. Likewise, a funding application may need a separate university admission or course nomination.

After identifying an admissible programme, compare its actual costs, scholarship route and visa sequence. This prevents an offer from becoming unusable because a financial or immigration requirement was left too late.

Frequently asked questions

Does the DAAD admission database guarantee admission?+

No. It provides orientation. The selected German higher education institution makes the final decision for the exact programme.

What is a VPD?+

A VPD is a preliminary review document issued by uni-assist for certain universities. You normally submit it in a separate direct university application before the deadline.

How long does a VPD take?+

uni-assist states that VPD processing usually takes four to six weeks after the complete online application is submitted.

Do all international applicants need Studienkolleg?+

No. It depends on the school qualification, prior study, intended subject and the institution's decision. Check the admission database and programme instructions.
