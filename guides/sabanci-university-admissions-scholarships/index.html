Sabancı University admissions and scholarships

Sabancı funding depends on whether you are applying for a bachelor’s degree or an academic graduate programme. Start with that distinction before comparing any advertised scholarship amount.

Updated 29 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

International undergraduate applicants who meet the requirements are automatically considered for admission scholarships. Academic graduate funding has separate award categories; do not transfer graduate stipends or benefits to an undergraduate offer.

Next step: Choose the undergraduate or graduate route, then compare the written package and remaining cost.

Undergraduate admission scholarships

Sabancı’s international scholarship page says eligible applicants are considered automatically, including those who leave the optional scholarship section blank. The committee considers academic evidence, and a given exam score does not guarantee a particular scholarship.

Organise your evidence by what it proves: school performance, subject preparation and independently verifiable achievements. An award certificate without context tells less than the result, competition level and your own contribution. Keep optional material relevant to the degree rather than uploading a large unsorted portfolio.

Official reference: Sabancı international scholarship policy

2026/27 undergraduate tuition and a net-fee example

The international undergraduate fee page lists USD 36,500 annually for 2026/27, with tuition-waiver options including 25%, 40%, 50%, 60% and 75%. Accommodation awards are a separate coverage question.

Illustrative calculation using that published fee: a confirmed 50% tuition waiver would leave USD 18,250 of annual tuition before other charges. This demonstrates the calculation; it is not an award prediction. Your issued offer decides the actual amount.

Academic graduate funding: read the category and months

The university’s 2026/27 graduate page separates full, project and tuition-waiver support, alongside other named awards. Professional graduate programmes are excepted from its general academic-funding statement.

For the full-scholarship category, the published master’s stipend is TRY 43,500 for 10 months; the doctorate category lists TRY 67,500 for 12 months. These are specific categories, not a promise to every admitted student. Read room availability and other benefit conditions in the official table.

Build a graduate plan around supervisor or research fit, the programme’s document list and the category actually offered. A higher listed maximum in a different scholarship is not evidence of what your department will award.

Official reference: Sabancı: 2026/27 graduate scholarship categories

Prepare an application that can be evaluated

Use the official admissions route for your degree. For a bachelor’s application, match your qualification to the published accepted exam/diploma list. For a research degree, follow the chosen graduate programme’s requirements and identify the work you want to pursue.

Keep a document inventory with the issue date, translation status and portal destination. If you are completing a qualification, confirm whether interim results are accepted and when the final document is due. The 2026/27 fee table does not itself announce a 2027 application deadline.

  3. 3Prepare a concise academic record and relevant supporting evidence.

Housing and campus planning

Evaluate the actual campus location in Tuzla when comparing a room elsewhere in Istanbul. A lower rent can be offset by daily travel cost and time, particularly if your timetable finishes late.

Ask for the covered room type, occupied months, summer arrangements and deposit treatment. If the award includes a room, confirm whether you must apply through a separate housing system. If it does not, request a current quote rather than assuming a graduate benefit is shared by undergraduate students.

Frequently asked questions

Does Sabancı need a separate undergraduate scholarship application?+

Its reviewed international scholarship page says eligible applicants are automatically considered. The optional scholarship indication does not replace the admission requirements or guarantee an award.

Is every Sabancı scholarship fully funded?+

No. Undergraduate tuition waivers and graduate award categories differ. Compare tuition, stipend, housing and other benefits in the specific written offer.

Are the published graduate stipend figures for all master’s programmes?+

No. The quoted figures refer to the 2026/27 full-scholarship category. Professional programmes and other funding categories have different treatment.
