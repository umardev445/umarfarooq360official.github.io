Germany deadline guide

Germany university application deadlines for 2027 entry

15 July and 15 January are common reference dates, not national guarantees. Your university, course, applicant category and VPD route decide the deadline that applies.

Updated 13 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

The short answer

Many courses use 15 July for a winter-semester start and 15 January for a summer-semester start, but master's, Studienkolleg and applicant-specific deadlines can be earlier or different.

Next step: Open the chosen programme's current application page and work backwards at least eight weeks; add extra time if a VPD is required.

Build a sixteen-week application timeline

A safe plan begins before the portal opens. Qualification research, language tests, certified translations and programme-specific documents can take longer than the final upload.

uni-assist recommends applying at least eight weeks before the deadline so missing-document issues can be identified. Treat that as a minimum planning buffer rather than permission to delay preparation.

  2. 212+ weeks: order documents and translations
  3. 38+ weeks: submit uni-assist or request VPD
  4. 4Before deadline: complete the university application

A VPD creates two deadlines

First submit a complete VPD request through uni-assist. After the evaluation, download the VPD and submit it with the direct university application before that institution's deadline.

uni-assist states that a VPD is normally valid for one year from issue, but it is issued for a chosen university. Recheck the programme and intake instructions before reusing it.

Frequently asked questions

Is 15 July the deadline for every German university?+

No. It is a common date for many winter-semester courses, but programmes and applicant categories can use earlier or different deadlines.

Is 15 January always the summer-semester deadline?+

No. It is a common reference date, and many degrees do not offer a summer intake. Check the exact course page.

When should I apply through uni-assist?+

uni-assist recommends applying at least eight weeks before the university deadline. Begin document and language preparation earlier.

Does receiving a VPD complete my application?+

No. For a VPD route, you normally still submit a separate direct application to the university before its deadline.
