Italy university application fees: amounts, refunds and payment steps

Compare what a payment actually buys: a programme assessment, several course choices or an entrance test. Keep these costs separate from tuition and enrolment.

Updated 28 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Italian university application fees depend on the institution, programme and payment period. Verified examples include Padua’s EUR 60 application route and Pavia’s EUR 35 per programme. A payment may be non-refundable, and an alternative test fee must not automatically be added twice.

Separate application, test, enrolment and tuition charges

An application or assessment payment funds the evaluation process; it does not reserve an offer or pay the annual university contribution. An entrance-test provider can charge separately, while some university routes substitute a particular test procedure for the usual application payment.

Check the fee period before choosing when to pay

PoliMi distinguishes an early payment period of 1 October–1 December from the standard period beginning 2 December in the reviewed master’s procedure. Use the dates for the selected call and complete submission as instructed; paying earlier does not cure an ineligible application.

If an application allows two programme choices for one payment, record those choices within that application. If another institution charges per programme, two applications mean two charges. Do not compare the totals without this distinction.

Official reference: PoliMi master's deadlines and fee periods · Pavia applicant FAQ

A shortlist budget with no hidden double counting

Original example: a student chooses two Pavia programmes. At the documented EUR 35 per programme, the assessment cost is 2 × 35 = EUR 70. Add language tests, translations and payment-provider charges only when actually required and evidenced.

Suppose the same student also makes one eligible Padua application under the EUR 60 route. The combined university application budget is EUR 130. This is an illustrative combination, not a package price, acceptance prediction or total cost of studying in Italy.

Worked example

Two Pavia programmes (EUR 70) + one Padua application under the specified route (EUR 60) = EUR 130. Tuition, travel and living costs remain separate.

Official reference: Padua English application instructions · Pavia applicant FAQ

Refunds and waivers: confirm them before paying

A rejection does not automatically produce a refund. Where the call says non-refundable, budget the payment as a cost even if admission is unsuccessful. An annual tuition waiver does not necessarily waive an application fee.

For an unclear rule, ask the admissions or fee office about your exact programme and academic year. Request the official refund/waiver instructions rather than relying on another applicant’s transaction. Do not send money to someone claiming to guarantee an exemption.

  • Confirm whether the call explicitly allows a waiver.
  • Read what happens after withdrawal, rejection or duplicate payment.
  • Keep any written approval and its conditions.
  • Pay through the university’s documented channel.

When a payment fails or the portal still shows unpaid

Keep the application reference, payment time, amount and transaction status. Check the university’s stated processing instructions before making another payment. A bank debit and a university receipt can describe different stages of the same transaction.

Contact the published admissions/payment office with the relevant reference and a suitably redacted receipt. Do not share card numbers or login credentials. If a duplicate payment occurred, follow the institution’s refund process; this guide cannot promise a reversal.

Bologna and no-fee claims need programme-level evidence

Do not assign Bologna one application price across all degrees. A course can have its own selection, test or application procedure. The fixed annual component shown on Bologna’s tuition page is not a university-wide application fee.

Likewise, a no-application-fee scholarship is not proof that every associated university course has free admission assessment. Compare the award form, university application and tests separately.

Frequently asked questions

Are Italian university application fees refundable?+

There is no universal rule. Padua and the cited PoliTO route describe non-refundable fees; read your own programme’s conditions before paying.

Does Pavia charge once for all choices?+

The reviewed FAQ states EUR 35 for each programme, with up to three choices per intake.

Should I add both PoliMi payment amounts?+

No. The early and standard amounts depend on the payment period; they are not a combined fee.

Is Universitaly pre-enrolment a paid university application?+

No. Universitaly pre-enrolment is free and separate from a university’s own application or assessment charges.
