128 UCAS points in grades: combinations and offer checks

Find what 128 points can look like, then check the route your university actually accepts. An equal tariff total does not make every qualification interchangeable.

Updated 26 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

128 UCAS points can be ABB at A level or DDM in a BTEC National Extended Diploma; mixed qualifications can also total 128 when accepted. The tables below separate exact tariff matches from qualifications that sit above or below the target.

Do not round a T Level Merit up to 128

A full T Level Merit is 120, while Distinction is 144. There is no full-award grade at 128. A course asking for a tariff minimum may describe a particular T Level route separately; read that route rather than inventing an intermediate grade.

For mixed programmes, decide whether every included qualification is accepted before adding the total. Points above the headline minimum can still leave a named subject or qualification-size condition unmet.

Worked example

BBB totals 120. An EPQ at E adds 8 and reaches 128 if it is counted. That does not make BBB plus EPQ E equivalent to an offer explicitly requiring ABB in three A levels; only an accepted alternative written by the provider can establish that.

Turn the number into an offer checklist

Write the actual offer next to your predicted or achieved record. Give each separate condition its own line so that a high total cannot conceal a missing requirement. If a condition excludes a qualification, calculate a second total with that qualification removed.

Teaching example

A short question for the admissions team

I am considering your course and expect approximately 128 tariff points from the following exact qualifications and grades: [list]. Your course page states [copy condition]. Does this combination meet the accepted qualification route, and are there required subjects or units I should check?

If your result is below the stated condition

Check whether the course provides an alternative qualification route or a contextual offer that actually applies to you. A lower offer is not automatic. If results have already been issued, read the status in your UCAS account and contact the provider about the outcome; a calculator cannot change that decision.

Use nearby grade totals to understand the arithmetic, not to predict which university will accept a shortfall. Only the provider can confirm an exception, and requirements can differ between courses at the same university.

Frequently asked questions

What A-level grades are 128 UCAS points?+

ABB is one exact three-A-level combination. The combinations table shows other passing-grade routes to the same total.

Can different qualifications with the same points meet the same offer?+

Possibly, but not automatically. The course may restrict qualification type, size, subject, units or the source of the points.

Can I add an EPQ to close a points gap?+

Only count it towards a particular offer when the provider accepts it. A named grade offer may have a separate EPQ alternative or none at all.

Are these university entry requirements?+

No. These are tariff calculations and offer-reading examples. Check the current course page and your actual offer for binding conditions.
