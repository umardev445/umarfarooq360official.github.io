Germany student visa guide

Germany student visa requirements and document checklist

Your responsible German mission and the Consular Services Portal provide the binding checklist. Use this guide to organise admission, finances and evidence without treating a generic list as a visa guarantee.

Updated 21 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Use the checklist from the German mission responsible for your residence and visa route. Admission evidence, financial proof and insurance must satisfy that route; a university offer alone does not establish visa eligibility.

Next step: Open the official mission instructions, record the document format and appointment process, then check financing against the published current requirement.

Keep three document decisions separate

Do not transfer funds based only on an agent’s checklist. Confirm which proof the official route accepts and keep account fees, insurance and arrival costs in the wider budget. Germany’s official study-visa portal gives €11,904 as the blocked-account minimum for 2026; this does not confirm a future intake’s amount.

Confirm the visa route and responsible mission

Applicants who require a national visa should use the German mission responsible for their place of residence. The Consular Services Portal supports online applications for study purposes, while an in-person appointment can still be required for original documents, biometrics and payment.

Do not apply through a different mission merely because its appointment calendar appears faster. Check nationality-based exemptions and your own residence situation on the official visa route.

Build the document file from the official checklist

The mission's portal or local website decides the exact format, copy, translation and appointment requirements. Use these categories to organise the file, then replace them with the binding local checklist.

Understand proof of financing before transferring money

Official guidance lists several possible forms of financial proof, including a blocked account, a recognised scholarship, a declaration of commitment and other accepted evidence. A blocked account is one route, not a fee paid to the German government.

The official study-visa guidance lists at least €11,904 for 2026 for a standard twelve-month study route. Amounts can change and other residence purposes use different rates, so verify the current figure for your purpose and mission before transferring funds.

Choose a blocked-account provider carefully

The Federal Foreign Office does not recommend a particular provider. Compare identity checks, opening time, setup and monthly fees, transfer method, refund procedure and customer support on the provider's current terms.

Monthly withdrawals are restricted so the required support remains available over the stay. Keep accessible money outside the account for deposits, travel and other costs due before the first release.

Submit, attend and track the application

Upload accurate documents through the stated portal, respond to preliminary-check messages and attend the required appointment with the requested originals. A complete upload does not guarantee approval or a particular processing time.

The mission can involve the foreigners authority at the study location. Do not purchase non-refundable travel based only on an expected decision date.

Plan the monthly budget after arrival

The official blocked amount is a financing threshold, not a personalised cost-of-living prediction. Housing and setup costs can make the real budget higher, especially at the start of the stay.

Use editable estimates for rent, food, insurance, transport, semester contributions and tuition. Compare the monthly total with the expected blocked-account release and confirmed scholarship support.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Germany blocked account for students in 2026?+

The official study-visa guidance lists at least €11,904 for 2026 for the standard twelve-month route. Verify the current amount for your purpose and responsible mission before transferring money.

Is a blocked account the only proof of financing?+

No. Official guidance also describes recognised scholarships, declarations of commitment and other accepted routes. The responsible mission decides what applies to the case.

Can I complete the entire visa process online?+

The Consular Services Portal supports online submission and preliminary checks, but an in-person appointment can still be required for originals, biometrics and the fee.

Does university admission guarantee the student visa?+

No. Admission supports the study purpose, while the visa authority separately assesses the complete application and applicable requirements.
