Italy IELTS requirements, accepted tests and MOI rules

Build an Italy application in the correct order and verify English evidence at programme level. This guide explains where IELTS, another certificate or prior English-medium education may fit.

Updated 25 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

The short answer

Italy has no single IELTS score. Polimi’s master’s language page lists IELTS at least 6 or PTE Academic at least 59, checked 25 October 2026. Other degrees set their own certificate thresholds or exemptions; a Pakistani MOI letter is not a universal waiver.

Padua's new exemption list: do not reuse old MOI advice

The September 2026 exemption PDF lists specific countries and entry qualifications. A degree taught in English in Pakistan is not automatically an exempt qualification. Check the linked PDF and obtain confirmation where your qualification is unclear.

Keep admission-language proof separate from consular visa requirements. A university exemption does not override the competent mission's checklist.

Bachelor's and master's academic admission

A bachelor's or single-cycle application with a foreign qualification normally requires a school history equivalent to at least 12 years, subject prerequisites and any course-specific test or ranking. Applicants from shorter school systems should read the university's permitted compensating study or foundation routes.

A master's application is assessed against the prior degree, subjects, credits, academic performance and any portfolio or programme-specific evidence. Polimi tells foreign-degree applicants to check country-specific qualification and GPA requirements before submitting its master's application.

Application documents to prepare

Use the selected programme's checklist. Common items include passport, qualification or current enrolment evidence, transcript, grading information, language proof, translations and programme-specific statements or portfolio. A positive initial evaluation can still require original, verified or recognition documents for enrolment.

Names and dates should match the passport. Upload complete scans in the required format and keep originals available. Never edit a transcript or create a replacement certificate yourself.

  • Passport
  • Final or provisional qualification
  • Transcript and grading scale
  • Accepted language evidence
  • Certified translation where required
  • Programme-specific CV, statement or portfolio
  • Recognition or authenticity evidence requested for enrolment

Universitaly pre-enrolment comes after the university route

Universitaly explains that applicants first choose a programme and contact or apply to the higher education institution. After the university process, eligible non-EU applicants living abroad complete online pre-enrolment and upload the records required for the institution's validation.

The validated pre-enrolment supports the visa application but does not create a right to a visa. Follow the competent embassy or consulate for the current appointment, financial, accommodation, insurance and document requirements.

A six-stage application tracker

Give each stage its own status and deadline so one completed form is not mistaken for the whole process.

  • 1. Programme and qualification fit
  • 2. University admission application
  • 3. Scholarship or fee-support application
  • 4. Admission decision and conditions
  • 5. Universitaly pre-enrolment where applicable
  • 6. Visa, travel and final enrolment

Ask admissions a precise question

State your programme, intake, qualification country, test name or proposed alternative and certificate issue date. Ask whether it meets the requirement and when it must be uploaded. Save the written response with the call; it is more useful than a general claim that the university accepts MOI.

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score do I need for Italy?+

It depends on the university and route. The selected current comparison gives Padua B2 IELTS 6.0 and Polimi MSc IELTS 6; your programme and certificate validity still need checking.

Does Padua accept a Pakistani MOI letter automatically?+

No. Do not rely on the older general MOI description. Use Padua's current exemption PDF and confirm your exact qualification.

Can I use PTE Academic?+

Selected current routes list it: Padua B2 at 65 and Polimi foreign-qualification MSc at 59. Check your own degree and the current certificate list.

Is the university English rule also the visa rule?+

No. Check admission and the competent Italian diplomatic mission's visa requirements separately.
