Undergraduate scholarships for Pakistani students: routes and requirements

Plan a first degree abroad around your qualification, nomination requirements and the costs the award actually covers. A scholarship deadline may be only the final stage of a longer process.

Updated 29 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Pakistani undergraduate applicants can research international university awards and applicable government routes, but admission and funding often require different steps. Pearson needs school nomination; Berea assesses international admission and aid together; Oxford funding follows an earlier admission application. Check the stage you can still complete.

Funding for study inside Pakistan

Pearson: a remaining deadline can have expired prerequisites

The published 2027 entry sequence gives school nomination by 9 October 2026, the required university admission application by 16 October and the scholarship form by 6 November. On 28 October, a new applicant who missed the earlier steps should not assume that the last date creates a fresh opportunity.

Already-nominated applicants should check their actual invitation, programme selection and submission requirements. A school nomination is a prerequisite, not a scholarship award. Review the university’s exact student-profile rules and funding conditions.

Official reference: University of Toronto Pearson

Berea: substantial support still needs a cash plan

Berea’s international guidance describes combined support for listed educational and living costs, but also explains a deposit and personal-cost expectations. Its cited USD 2,200 deposit has an assistance process for those unable to pay. Read that process rather than describing the route as requiring no money at any stage.

Follow the current English-score and document checklist. Berea also sets its own rules on outside assistance with the personal essay: the applicant must comply with those instructions. A general scholarship writing template is not permission to have another person or AI write an application essay.

Official reference: Berea international applicants

Oxford: admission and funding do not share one deadline

Oxford’s 2027 undergraduate funding instructions describe a January scholarship stage. Admission has an earlier sequence. Someone who has not completed the required admission application cannot treat a later funding date as a reopened course application.

Open the particular award, check Pakistan’s inclusion and any residence, financial-need, subject and first-degree restrictions. Do not combine several Oxford awards into one guaranteed fully funded package.

Official reference: Oxford undergraduate funding

FSc, A levels and entry tests: keep requirements specific

Universities decide how they assess a Pakistani school qualification and the subjects required for a course. A grade threshold from one institution is not a universal percentage for foreign scholarships. Record the qualification, subject prerequisites and graduation timing for each actual programme.

HEC’s applicable foreign-funded routes can use USAT for undergraduate selection; other institutions use their own admission process. Do not label every overseas scholarship as requiring USAT, SAT or one English test. Use the current owner and programme instructions.

Official reference: HEC Learning Opportunities Abroad

Full-cost, full-tuition and partial support mean different things

Full tuition describes university fees, not automatically rent, food, travel, insurance, tests or a visa. A living allowance may be paid only after enrolment. Record the benefit and its start date, then identify what your household must finance before arrival.

Frequently asked questions

Can I start Pearson only because the November form deadline remains?+

Only if the university confirms you satisfied its earlier nomination and admission requirements. The last form date is not a new first-stage deadline.

Is every full-tuition scholarship fully funded?+

No. Living costs, travel, tests and other charges can remain.

Does every undergraduate award require USAT?+

No. Use USAT where the applicable HEC call requires it; direct university and other government routes can differ.

Can a later Oxford scholarship deadline replace the admission deadline?+

No. Admission and funding have separate requirements and stages.
