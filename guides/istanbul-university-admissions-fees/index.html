Istanbul University international admissions and fees

This guide is for Istanbul University (İstanbul Üniversitesi). Use the correct institution, faculty and international admission category before comparing requirements or fees.

Updated 29 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Istanbul University’s 2026 international admission record lists TR-YÖS and several international qualifications, including SAT, ACT, A-levels and IB. Its 2026/27 fee schedule is faculty-specific, with annual and semester amounts shown separately.

Keep the institution and application route clear

Istanbul University, Istanbul University-Cerrahpaşa and Istanbul Technical University are different institutions. A result, fee or scholarship from one should not be attached to another.

International admission qualifications and 2026 timing

The official indexed 2026 record lists TR-YÖS sessions 2024/2, 2025/1, 2025/2 and 2026/1, alongside GCE A-levels, SAT, ACT, IB, French Baccalaureate, Abitur and Austrian Matura. A listed qualification must still satisfy the programme’s conditions.

The 2026 preference window shown in that record was 13–20 July 2026 and has passed. Do not extend the deadline into the next year. Check the next named call for its qualification versions, quotas and sequence.

Keep a copy of the accepted-result rule with your score report. If a more recent exam session is not in the old list, that does not establish the rule for a future call; the new admissions announcement decides.

Read the fee table without doubling the wrong amount

The schedule states that half of the annual tuition is paid in fall and half in spring. It also says preparation students pay the fee set for their registered faculty/school/institute. Open/distance-education and other rows must be kept separate from a campus first-education degree.

Worked example: the Faculty of Science row is TRY 166,635 annually. Two semester payments of TRY 83,317.50 already produce that annual amount. Multiplying the annual figure by two would double-count tuition. Add accommodation and other expenses separately.

Language and document checks before accepting

Use the exact programme language and the faculty’s registration instructions. Do not infer that a degree is taught in English because the university has an English admissions page. If a preparation route is required, include its time and tuition treatment in the plan.

Build two document sets: the evidence used for application evaluation and the originals or equivalence evidence needed for final registration. Confirm the delivery deadline for any missing final diploma. School-level MEB Denklik and university admission are separate processes.

An Istanbul offer needs a campus-specific living plan

Record where your faculty teaches, then compare actual room locations and travel routes. The cost of living near a different university in Istanbul is not automatically a good budget for your programme.

Original example: two rooms have the same monthly price, but one requires a long transfer-heavy commute and does not issue acceptable residence-address evidence. The cheaper-looking contract may create both travel and registration problems. Check these conditions before reserving.

Frequently asked questions

How much is Istanbul University tuition for international students?+

It depends on the faculty and route. Selected 2026/27 first-education examples range from TRY 95,220 annually for Letters to TRY 476,100 for the Istanbul Faculty of Medicine; use the full official schedule for your faculty.

Does Istanbul University accept SAT or ACT?+

Both appear in the official indexed 2026 international qualification list. Check the exact new call and programme conditions before treating a result as eligible.

Is Istanbul University the same as Istanbul University-Cerrahpaşa?+

No. Use the correct institution’s admissions office, programme list and fee schedule.
