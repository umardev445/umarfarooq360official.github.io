Current call and degree route

The reviewed USEFP page funds master’s and PhD study. It describes 16 years of education for master’s and 18 for PhD applicants, and excludes clinical medicine. MBA and Public Policy/Administration routes require two years of relevant work experience. Funding includes tuition, textbooks, airfare, a living stipend and health insurance, subject to the programme budget.

Tests, transcripts and references are separate tasks

The reviewed call requires GRE General, with a stated minimum of 145 in each of the two scored sections and a stronger relevant-section score. Its package includes three references, a GRE report and transcripts with the grading-scale explanation. Selected candidates take TOEFL. The call allows issuing-authority-attested degree/transcript evidence at application, while preferring HEC attestation. Read the complete eligibility and exclusion list, including nationality/residence conditions, directly on USEFP.

Create a private submission folder with separate files for academic history, tests, referees and essay evidence. Label files by document and version rather than using names such as final-final. Record whether a requirement is ready, requested or blocked. This is an organisation method, not a substitute for the next application’s upload instructions.

Build an evidence argument before writing

Start with a concrete academic or public problem you want to understand. Identify an experience that made the problem visible, what you did, the limitation you encountered and the training you need. A statement that merely praises the United States or asks for funding does not establish that connection.

A useful planning row contains: problem, your action, observable result, lesson, proposed study and intended contribution. If you cannot support a result, describe the work honestly without invented numbers. Where a goal depends on an institution or employer, explain the first feasible step rather than promising a national transformation.

Teaching example

Illustrative evidence note, not an application answer

During a course project, I compared two low-cost sensing approaches and found that the noisier measurements limited the conclusion. My next training need is stronger experimental design and signal analysis. I should now identify the actual work I completed and the relevant study direction before turning this note into an essay.

Fictional teaching example. Replace it with your own experience and follow the current prompt.

Choose a referee who can evaluate the evidence

Ask people who observed your work and can explain its quality. Share a short evidence sheet, your study direction and the submission deadline. Agree their availability before entering contact details in a portal. A famous name with no knowledge of your work may produce a less useful letter than a direct supervisor.

The USEFP FAQ explains that placement is handled by experienced placement professionals; personal university preferences are not the same as a guaranteed destination. It also clarifies degree-stage and test questions. Check its current wording when your circumstances do not fit a simple checklist.

Preparation checklist while the call is closed

Choose tasks that remain useful even if the next call changes. An accurate transcript, a realistic study rationale and referees who know your work are more durable than an essay written for an old prompt.
