CIMEA vs Declaration of Value for studying in Italy

Confirm the evidence your receiving institution accepts for your qualification and enrolment stage. The document’s issuer and purpose matter more than which route another applicant used.

Updated 28 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

A Declaration of Value is issued through the competent Italian diplomatic/consular route and describes a foreign qualification. CIMEA provides comparability or verification statements. Neither alone guarantees recognition, admission or a visa; the receiving authority decides the accepted evidence.

The qualification country can determine consular jurisdiction

The competent Italian mission for a Declaration of Value is connected to the country where the qualification was issued, under the applicable consular instructions. Citizenship and qualification country can differ. A Pakistani citizen with a degree from another country should not automatically copy a Pakistan-issued-degree checklist.

Read the competent mission’s current appointment, translation and legalisation instructions, then confirm what the receiving university needs. This guide does not set one global fee or processing time.

Official reference: Italian Ministry: Declaration of Value

Comparability and verification answer different questions

A statement about a qualification’s level and a check of its authenticity address different needs. Do not treat the word CIMEA as a single product that automatically includes every service. The receiving institution should identify the evidence it wants and the stage when it wants it.

Before ordering, copy the exact wording from the programme or enrolment instructions. If it names comparability, verification, both, a Declaration of Value or an alternative, keep that distinction visible. A general explanation of services does not establish what your particular university will accept.

Three applicant scenarios

Scenario A: an applicant sees a document requirement only in final-enrolment instructions. They ask whether it must be completed before admission or can follow an offer. They use the confirmed timing instead of paying immediately because another student did so.

Scenario B: a university names two statements. The applicant checks the qualification, current official service package and upload stage. They do not assume that ordering one statement automatically fulfils the second request.

Scenario C: the programme permits a listed alternative. The applicant compares the accepted routes and timing after confirming that the alternative applies to their qualification country. These are fictional decision scenarios, not promises about a named university’s policy.

Ask for clarification with a useful email

A precise question can prevent duplicate purchases. State the programme, intake, qualification country and document instruction. Ask about the evidence and timing, rather than asking whether CIMEA is generally compulsory in Italy.

Teaching example

Fictional clarification email

Dear Admissions Team, I am preparing an application for the stated programme and intake. My qualification was issued in [country]. Your instructions mention [exact document wording]. Could you confirm which statement or accepted alternative is required for this qualification, and whether it must be supplied at application or final enrolment? I would also appreciate the official document page for the procedure. Thank you.

Replace the placeholders with your own case. Send to the institution’s published contact, not an unverified intermediary.

Record fees, processing and dependencies without guessing

Current fees, processing routes and document lists must be checked on the service or competent mission. We do not publish a universal processing time or fee because the required service and case can differ. An advertised standard period is not a guaranteed completion date.

Record the ordering date, reference number, requested evidence, estimated processing information, university upload deadline and a contact for queries. If the institution changes the requirement, seek clarification before ordering a second route. Keep the original instructions and response together.

Final checks before submitting the result

Confirm that the name, qualification, issuing institution and document type match your application. Read whether the university needs an uploaded copy, original or verification through its own channel. Do not assume a document accepted by one institution is accepted everywhere.

A qualification-information document does not itself confer admission, a scholarship or a visa. Continue the remaining programme and immigration tasks separately.

Frequently asked questions

Is CIMEA the same as a Declaration of Value?+

No. They are different qualification-information routes. The university or competent authority decides which evidence it accepts or requires.

Do all Italian universities require CIMEA?+

No. Requirements vary by university, programme, qualification country and stage of enrolment. Read the exact current instructions.

Should I order CIMEA before admission?+

Only when the university says it is required at that stage or the processing timeline makes early action necessary. Confirm the exact service first.

Does a Declaration of Value guarantee recognition?+

No. It supplies information about a foreign qualification; the receiving institution makes the relevant academic decision.
