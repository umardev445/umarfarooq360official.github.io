ISEE parificato for international students: documents and steps

Find the economic-assessment route your university actually accepts before collecting, translating or paying for documents. This guide separates the assessment from the scholarship application.

Updated 28 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

The short answer

ISEE parificato is an equivalent household assessment used by some institutions for overseas finances. It is not itself a scholarship application. Your institution or regional authority decides the accepted assessment, documents and reference years.

Next step: Before ordering translations, confirm the accepted assessor or direct-document route, household members and income/asset reference dates in the current call.

Which route applies to you?

Nationality alone does not answer this question. Where your household lives and where its income and assets are held are more useful starting points. Tell the office about mixed Italian and overseas finances rather than leaving one part out.

Padua and ER.GO are different processes

Padua directs eligible international applicants to its affiliated CAF for equivalent ISEE, followed by the Uniweb concession request. ER.GO uses its own foreign-document submission route. Do not buy a generic certificate and assume both authorities will accept it.

  • Confirm the exact service on the official website, not an agency's screenshot.
  • Keep evidence of assessment and the separate benefit application.
  • Ask whether originals are required later and where to retain them.

Keep three dates separate

Write down the deadline for the online benefit application, the deadline for submitting financial evidence and any deadline for correcting a deficient file. A later document-upload date does not necessarily extend the original scholarship application.

As an ER.GO example, its official instructions ask for current-year issued documentation describing the previous year's income and year-end assets. Reopen the annual call before ordering records; do not reuse this example as a national rule.

A practical sequence for your family

First make an inventory of available records. Next send a concise question to the authorised office about any missing category. Only then obtain the required versions, arrange accepted translation/authentication, upload through the stated route and save the confirmation.

Use consistent passport names across the file. If records use an older spelling, explain it through the accepted evidence instead of changing an official scan. Keep a simple log showing issuer, issue date, reference year, translation status and upload receipt.

  • One folder per application year.
  • One row per document, with the person it relates to.
  • One unresolved-question list for the authority.
  • No passwords or unnecessary bank documents sent to strangers.

Zero income and independence are not shortcuts

Zero earnings are different from zero assets. A family member can have no salary while holding savings or property. Declare the full position in the format the authority requires.

Living away from your parents is not, by itself, proof of financial independence. Ask for the published independence test and keep evidence for every condition. Do not omit the parental household just because you currently rent a room elsewhere.

After submission: follow the file, not just the ranking

Check the official portal and inbox for clarification requests. Save the submitted application and final assessment separately. If a document is rejected, ask what defect needs correcting and whether a correction period is still available.

Build a budget that works while the decision is pending. An economic assessment can support an application; it does not promise a grant, an available room or an immediate payment.

Frequently asked questions

Is ISEE parificato a scholarship?+

No. It assesses the household's financial position. You still need to apply for the specific benefit and meet its other conditions.

Can I use the same certificate at every university?+

Do not assume so. The authorised assessor, document years and accepted submission routes can differ.

Can I estimate my ISEE with a GPA or study-cost calculator?+

No. These calculators do not assess household income and assets under Italian financial-aid rules.
