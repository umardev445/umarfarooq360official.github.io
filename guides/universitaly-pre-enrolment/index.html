Universitaly pre-enrolment: from university instructions to validation

Understand what to prepare and who handles each step. This is a planning guide, not a reproduction of every portal screen or a substitute for your university's intake instructions.

Updated 21 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

The short answer

For the applicable international-student route, Universitaly pre-enrolment is a separate step from university admission. The institution validates the request; the competent Italian diplomatic mission decides the visa. Validation does not guarantee a visa.

Next step: Follow your university’s pre-enrolment instructions, prepare consistent programme and identity details, then save the submitted request and monitor corrections.

What to do when the request is not yet validated

The official process assigns different decisions to the university and the diplomatic mission. There is no universal processing-time promise in this guide. Check the portal message and contact the office that owns the next action instead of submitting duplicate requests without instructions.

Step 1: confirm that this route applies

Check the instructions for your citizenship, residence status, qualification and course. Do not copy the process of an EU applicant if you are a non-EU applicant living abroad.

Before using the portal, ask the university when it expects you to submit and whether an admission condition must be completed first. The institution's intake-specific instructions should resolve the timing, not a general video.

Use a working checklist so that the university application and pre-enrolment request describe the same person and programme. Check scan quality before starting.

Step 3: create your account and complete the request

Start from Universitaly's official website and follow its registration/login link. Record your credentials securely. Do not share a login with anyone claiming they can guarantee validation.

Choose the pre-enrolment route and follow the current form. Compare course names carefully when similar names appear. Upload the documents agreed with your institution and review the completed request before submission. Portal labels can change; use university help when the screen differs from an old tutorial.

Step 4: save proof and monitor corrections

After submitting, retain the application reference and any downloadable receipt. Check the portal and university email for requests to correct or supplement documents.

A request for correction is a task to resolve, not a reason to create several conflicting applications. Describe the precise issue to the institution: application reference, course, field or document affected and the message displayed. Never include your password in a support email.

Step 5: understand validation and the visa handoff

Universitaly explains that the institution validates a positively assessed request for the relevant diplomatic mission. This does not give the applicant a right to a visa. Use the mission's current requirements for the next stage.

Do not make non-refundable travel commitments solely because a portal status changed. Also check whether enrolment requires original qualifications or further verification after arrival. There is no universal guaranteed validation duration; request an update from the institution if a stated deadline is approaching.

Your final consistency check

Compare the university offer, submitted pre-enrolment request and visa file side by side. Correct material differences using the authorised route before relying on the documents.

  • Same passport identity.
  • Same programme, institution and intake.
  • Required conditions completed or clearly recorded.
  • Latest receipt and university messages saved.
  • Visa checklist taken from the competent mission.
  • University arrival/enrolment tasks recorded separately.

Frequently asked questions

Is Universitaly my admission application?+

It does not replace the university's admission process. Follow the institution's instructions on when and how to pre-enrol.

Does validation guarantee a visa?+

No. The relevant Italian diplomatic mission makes the visa decision.

How long does validation take?+

There is no guaranteed national processing time. The university reviews the request; ask it about your file and any missing evidence.
