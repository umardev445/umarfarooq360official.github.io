DSU scholarships in Italy: eligibility, benefits and documents

DSU is a family of regional right-to-study benefits. Use this guide to identify the correct authority, prepare foreign financial documents and keep the scholarship deadline separate from admission.

Updated 28 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

The short answer

DSU scholarships are regional student-support schemes. Your authority and annual call determine income and asset limits, academic conditions, cash benefits, meals and housing; there is no single national application or guaranteed amount.

Next step: Choose the authority covering your institution, then check its current call before preparing family documents or requesting accommodation.

The status below concerns the named regional record only. Italy has separate regional competitions; use the authority comparison to choose Lazio DiSCo, DSU Toscana, ER.GO or EDISU.

Toscana 2026–27: degree categories have different dates

Official calendar checked 24 October 2026: DSU Toscana lists 7 September 2026 as the closed degree/master’s deadline, while its doctoral and specialisation category lists 16 November 2026 at 13:00. These are Toscana dates, not a national DSU deadline. Confirm the call and applicant category before acting.

Do not budget for a housing place simply because you applied: the authority describes availability and award-category conditions. Record tuition treatment, cash support, meals and housing separately, with a funding gap and a cash-flow plan.

First application or renewal: check which evidence is required

DSU Toscana’s 2026–2027 foreign-student instructions distinguish first-time applicants and specified changes of degree from some returning scholarship holders. Its renewal exception is conditional; it is not a rule for every region. Use the authority’s wording rather than recycling another student’s checklist.

Economic and academic conditions work together

A first-year application is normally assessed using economic conditions and the enrolment rules in the call. Continued or later-year benefits can depend on earning the required credits by specified dates. The exact thresholds, credit rules and exceptions must be taken from the current regional notice.

For 2026–2027, ER.GO published €25,000 ISEE and €50,000 ISPE access thresholds for its scholarship and accommodation benefits. Both values had to fall within the stated limits. These are ER.GO figures for a named year and should not be copied to another region or future call.

Documents for income and assets outside Italy

International applicants whose family unit has income or assets abroad must prove the household's composition and economic position according to the regional instructions. Typical categories include household members, annual income, buildings or land, and bank or other movable assets, but the call determines the precise reference year and evidence.

Documents may need issue by the competent authority, translation and legalisation or another accepted authentication. Prepare from the official checklist and keep names, dates and family relationships consistent across every document. A self-written statement is not a substitute when the call requires official evidence.

Student status can change the benefit

Regional calls can distinguish students living at home, commuting and living away from home. Accommodation evidence and deadlines can affect this classification. ER.GO explains that the assigned amount varies with domicile and provides specific guidance for international students with income and assets abroad.

Do not assume that selecting an away-from-home option is enough. Read what contract, duration, registration or residence evidence must be submitted and when.

Admission and DSU use separate clocks

A DSU deadline may arrive before enrolment is complete. Follow the call's rule for applicants awaiting admission and note any later deadline to finalise enrolment. Missing the scholarship deadline is not normally repaired by receiving a university offer later.

The main ER.GO scholarship deadline shown for 2026–2027 was 24 August 2026 at 16:00. Use it only as historical planning context and check the next official call for new dates.

What a DSU benefit may contain

Depending on the authority and student status, support may combine a monetary grant with catering, accommodation or other services. The headline amount can be affected by services provided and later verification. Read the payment schedule and conditions before building a budget.

Plan for tuition and mandatory university charges separately. A DSU application can interact with fee reductions, but the university explains how fees are assessed and when documents must be registered.

Plan money before the first payment

Make three separate columns: confirmed grant cash, services such as meals/housing and costs you must pay yourself. Keep deposits, travel and early rent in an arrival-cash budget. Do not count an unawarded benefit as money already available.

A provisional ranking or economic eligibility is not a guarantee of a room or immediate payment. Follow the award decision, required follow-up actions and published payment schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Is there one DSU application for all Italian universities?+

No. The responsible regional body or institution depends on where you study. Use the selected university's current funding page to find the correct authority.

Can I apply for DSU before university enrolment?+

Some calls permit an application while admission or enrolment is pending, followed by a later completion deadline. Follow the exact current call.

What is ISEE parificato or equivalent economic documentation?+

It is a process used to assess a household whose income or assets cannot be captured through the standard Italian ISEE. The institution or authorised service specifies the required foreign documents and calculation route.

Does DSU cover accommodation automatically?+

No. Accommodation can have its own request, availability, ranking and evidence rules. Read both the scholarship and housing sections of the call.

Can a consultant guarantee a DSU award?+

No. The competent body assesses the official application and documents. Use its portal and published contacts.
