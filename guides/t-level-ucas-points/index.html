T Level UCAS points: full awards and partial achievement

A full T Level has an overall grade and a tariff value. Check the overall result and core-grade band rather than adding component marks yourself.

Updated 26 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

A full T Level carries 168 points at Distinction*, 144 at Distinction, 120 at Merit, and either 96 or 72 at Pass depending on the core grade. Partial achievement needs a separate official lookup.

Next step: Confirm whether your record is a full T Level or partial achievement, then read the course requirements for the named subject and result.

Full T Level points by overall grade

The Department for Education lists the full-award tariff below. A Pass with core C or above has a different value from a Pass with core D or E. The core grade therefore matters even when two results both say Pass.

Why Pass can mean 96 or 72

Do not look up Pass without the associated core result. A full Pass in the higher core band gives 96 points, while the lower core band gives 72. The difference is 24 points; it is not a discretionary bonus a student can add to a record.

For a hypothetical minimum of 96 that accepts the relevant T Level, the higher-band full Pass reaches the numeric threshold and the lower band does not. That calculation does not confirm subject suitability or any additional conditions.

Worked example

A full T Level at Merit is 120 points. If an offer asks for 128, do not round Merit up to 128 or treat the difference as automatically waived. Ask admissions about the actual accepted route.

Partial achievement is a different lookup

Current Department for Education guidance describes tariff allocation for partial achievement where the placement and only one other component have been completed. The applicable entry depends on the achievement recorded. Do not borrow the full-award value or invent a universal core-only tariff.

Use the official UCAS lookup with your subject and exact result. Our simplified calculator supports full awards only; a missing partial-award option means it is outside this tool, not that it is worthless or rejected by every university.

Official reference: Department for Education: T Level tariff guidance · Official UCAS qualification calculator

Tariff comparison does not decide university acceptance

A full T Level is aligned with three A levels in tariff size, but the course may specify acceptable T Level subjects or additional academic preparation. Ask whether your particular pathway is accepted, which overall result is required, and whether separate Mathematics, science or GCSE conditions apply.

Frequently asked questions

How many points is T Level Merit?+

A Merit in a full T Level is 120 UCAS points under the published overall-grade tariff.

Why does T Level Pass have two values?+

A full Pass has 96 points when the core grade is C or above, and 72 when it is D or E.

Can I add the core and specialism points to the full award?+

Do not add component values to a full-award tariff. Use the official entry for the achievement actually awarded.

Do all universities accept my T Level?+

Acceptance depends on the institution, course and subject. A tariff value alone does not establish that a particular course accepts the qualification.
