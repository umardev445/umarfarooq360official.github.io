EPQ UCAS points: grades, combinations and offer rules

An EPQ has a published tariff value, but your chosen course decides how it treats that qualification. Compare the arithmetic and the offer wording separately.

Updated 26 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

EPQ grades A*, A, B, C, D and E carry 28, 24, 20, 16, 12 and 8 UCAS points respectively. Whether those points count towards a particular offer depends on the course.

Three different ways an offer can treat an EPQ

Do not turn a total into an offer interpretation. A points-based offer may accept the EPQ, a grade-based offer may have a stated EPQ alternative, and another offer may not count it. The official course requirements and your actual offer determine which applies.

A points match is not the same as a reduced-grade offer

Suppose an offer is written as AAB, or ABB plus a specified EPQ grade. You must satisfy the named alternative; a different set of qualifications with the same tariff total may not qualify. Likewise, adding an EPQ does not replace a specifically required grade in Chemistry or Mathematics unless the provider explicitly says it does.

Frequently asked questions

How many UCAS points is an EPQ A*?+

An A* in the EPQ is 28 UCAS points. A is 24, B is 20, C is 16, D is 12 and E is 8.

Does an EPQ always lower my offer?+

No. A reduction must be stated by the course or in the offer. The tariff value does not create a concession automatically.

Can CCC plus EPQ A total 120?+

Yes: 96 + 24 = 120 arithmetically. Check that the course counts the EPQ and accepts the qualification combination.

Does an EPQ replace an A level?+

It is a separate qualification. Its tariff value does not make it a replacement for a named A level or subject requirement.
