University of Turin admissions and scholarships for international students

Choose the correct UniTo application route, understand its cost and connect the programme offer to funding and enrolment without mixing different deadlines.

Updated 20 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

University of Turin admission, university scholarships and EDISU Piemonte are separate processes. The checked main 2026/27 application dates have passed. Use programme-specific calls to confirm a new intake rather than assuming the old dates repeat.

Next step: Choose the exact programme and applicant category on Apply@UniTo, then create separate records for admission, funding and Universitaly.

UniTo application facts and cycle status

The official 2026/27 international application page lists a €60 application allowing up to two programmes. This is an application charge, not the annual tuition bill. The main published postgraduate and undergraduate application periods below have ended. Restricted or later calls depend on the applicant category and programme.

Find your programme before evaluating eligibility

A university name does not identify an admissions route. Start with the degree title, level, teaching language and whether access is restricted. Then check whether you apply as a non-EU visa applicant, an EU/equivalent applicant or a person already legally resident in Italy. The permitted call can change with that category.

Compare the programme curriculum with your previous study. For a master’s course, identify relevant modules and academic preparation rather than assuming any bachelor’s degree is sufficient. For undergraduate entry, confirm that your school qualification satisfies the required education pathway; ask about a foundation route if it does not.

  • Exact programme title and campus recorded.
  • Qualification and subject requirements checked.
  • Applicant category and permitted call confirmed.
  • Teaching language and accepted evidence saved.

English and Italian requirements: use the actual course rules

There is no safe university-wide promise of admission without IELTS. An English-taught programme may accept specific tests or another documented route; the programme decides what evidence is valid. An English-medium degree letter is useful only when the relevant rules accept it.

The international admissions page separately explains Italian evidence for affected Italian-taught undergraduate and single-cycle applicants. Do not transfer those rules to every English-taught master’s programme. In your tracker, record the required language, proof type, score if specified, validity and date by which it must be supplied.

Build an application that can be evaluated

Make the academic record readable as a whole: qualification, transcript and any required explanation or translation. If the course asks about subject preparation, use the official module evidence instead of an unsupported claim that your degree is equivalent. Match names consistently across your passport and study records.

Keep a checklist per programme even where one portal allows two choices. A requirement belonging to the first choice may not satisfy the second. Before paying, verify that the correct programmes and category are selected and that you can meet the relevant call. Save the final receipt and monitor the portal for requests.

University funding and EDISU: two different routes

The university scholarship page lists different opportunities, including targeted awards and EDISU Piemonte. A named merit award can have a narrower degree, language or nationality scope than the university’s overall international admission. Do not assume a past Talents 4 UniTo call is open or available to every admitted student.

For EDISU, research the regional notice and financial documents independently. An academic offer is useful to your study plan, but it does not replace the benefit application. Compare the documents and timing while you are preparing admission rather than discovering a separate funding deadline afterwards.

Tuition and arrival costs: ask for the right calculation

Treat tuition assessment and scholarship selection as separate calculations. The university publishes international fee rules and reduction procedures; confirm which basis applies to your residence and family circumstances. An application fee does not lock in a particular tuition reduction.

Build a first-year budget with tuition, the relevant regional or administrative charges, rent, food, transport, document costs and an arrival reserve. A later award should reduce the budget only when its scope and timing are clear. Otherwise, keep a confirmed-funding column and a pending-funding column.

After an offer: Universitaly, originals and enrolment

For relevant non-EU students abroad, UniTo identifies a separate Universitaly pre-enrolment process. Admission and university validation do not guarantee a visa, and a visa does not remove the university’s remaining enrolment checks. Use the institution’s instructions and your competent consular authority together.

List what is still missing after the offer: final degree evidence, any accepted comparability documentation, language evidence, pre-enrolment and final enrolment steps. Ask for the precise deadline and accepted format for each. This is more useful than assuming that an acceptance PDF finishes the process.

Frequently asked questions

Are the University of Turin and Politecnico di Torino the same?+

No. They are separate universities with different programme catalogues, admissions systems and calls. Their students may research the same regional EDISU authority, but university applications remain separate.

What is the University of Turin application fee?+

The checked 2026/27 international application page states €60 for up to two programmes. Recheck the current portal before paying for a later cycle or a different application route.

Can I use one deadline for every UniTo programme?+

No. Degree, programme access rules and applicant category can change the permitted call. Keep a separate record for admission, scholarship and pre-enrolment.

Does an admission offer include an EDISU scholarship?+

No. EDISU has its own application, documents and conditions. Use the dedicated regional guide and current notice.
