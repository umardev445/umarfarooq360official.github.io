Politecnico di Torino admissions and scholarships: choose your application route

Plan a degree application to Politecnico di Torino with separate checks for the programme, permitted admission call, funding and post-offer requirements.

Updated 20 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Politecnico di Torino and the University of Turin are different institutions. PoliTO admissions depend on degree and applicant category. Its checked 2026/27 foreign-qualification master’s page lists a €50 application fee and a later call restricted to specified applicants and programmes.

Next step: Find your exact programme and whether your residence/category permits the call before paying or using a deadline.

Call eligibility matters more than the nearest date

The published 2026/27 master’s page contains different calls, revised dates and a later call for EU applicants and non-EU applicants residing in Italy. A student abroad who needs a visa cannot assume that the next visible deadline is available to them.

On 20 October 2026, the first two published calls have ended. The listed November call is upcoming and restricted; it is not a general invitation for all international applicants or all degrees.

Choose degree, programme and qualification route

Start from the official prospective-student entry point, then select bachelor’s or master’s instructions. Engineering, architecture and design applications can require different evidence and assessments. The master’s instructions for a non-Italian qualification are not a bachelor’s entrance-test guide.

Read the curriculum requirement alongside your transcript. For a technical master’s, identify relevant subject preparation and any additional assessment requested by the course. For design-related study, check whether a portfolio is required. A strong academic record is useful, but it cannot replace a missing compulsory prerequisite.

Official reference: PoliTO prospective-student routes

Language and documents: confirm the programme-specific evidence

Record the required teaching language and accepted proof for the selected programme. Do not apply one internet IELTS score to every course. Where exemptions exist, use the stated evidence and submit it in the required form; studying in English previously is not automatically an exemption.

Organise the qualification, transcript, required academic details and programme-specific materials before starting the application. Pay attention to the distinction between the university’s academic documentation and any extra documents requested by the consular authority later. Keep both tasks visible without claiming that every applicant must buy the same recognition document at the same stage.

Apply@PoliTO: payment and final submission are separate checks

The checked master’s guidance states a €50 non-refundable fee and directs candidates to confirm submission in the application system. Make time for both payment and the final submission step. A payment confirmation alone is not the same evidence as a submitted application.

If a payment behaves unexpectedly, check the official payment instructions and whether the first transaction was processed before trying repeatedly. Retain the application record and receipt together. After submission, monitor the relevant evaluation area and use the official support route if a required file or status is unclear.

Turn the admission result into a next action

An eligible result, a conditional offer and a negative decision have different consequences. If your degree is incomplete, identify what must be supplied before final enrolment. Do not assume an offer can be carried to another academic year without checking the institution’s deferral rule.

When a result is negative, read whether the issue is missing basic requirements or academic/curricular suitability. The remedy can differ, and a new upload is not always accepted after evaluation. A precise question citing your application and the university’s result wording is more useful than asking an unofficial adviser to predict admission.

Funding: compare university awards with EDISU Piemonte

PoliTO publishes scholarship and award opportunities with different target groups. A scholarship for a particular programme, nationality or project is not an entitlement for all international students. Check the named call before using an amount in your budget.

EDISU Piemonte is a separate regional application with economic, academic and enrolment rules. Keep the university award and EDISU on different tracker rows. For either route, record coverage, application deadline, acceptance conditions and whether support arrives as cash, a service or a fee adjustment.

After eligibility: Universitaly and final enrolment

For relevant non-EU applicants abroad, the university requires the separate pre-enrolment route on Universitaly. The official guidance states that admission does not guarantee a visa. Read the final enrolment requirements as well; travelling to Italy does not complete the academic administration.

Keep a post-offer list containing the final degree evidence, qualification checks, pre-enrolment, competent consular procedure and university enrolment actions. Use the exact dates attached to your call and applicant group. Another student’s deadline, even for the same university, may not be yours.

Frequently asked questions

Is the November PoliTO call open to all international applicants?+

No. The checked 2026/27 master’s page restricts that call to the specified EU/resident applicant groups and listed programmes. Check your category before relying on it.

What application fee does the checked master’s page state?+

It states a €50 non-refundable fee. This should not be copied onto exchange or bachelor’s routes without checking their separate instructions.

Does an eligible result guarantee funding?+

No. Academic admission, scholarship selection and regional EDISU benefits are separate decisions.

Is PoliTO the same as Politecnico di Milano?+

No. Politecnico di Torino is in Turin and Politecnico di Milano is a different institution. Use the correct university portal and programme call.
