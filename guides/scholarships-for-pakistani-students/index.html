Choose an undergraduate, master’s, MBA or research route

For undergraduate study, check school qualifications, eligible entry year and whether the award needs school nomination. Use the bachelor’s funding guide before borrowing a postgraduate application checklist.

For a master’s or MBA, check the exact course against the award’s eligible degrees and funding exclusions. A scholarship for master’s study does not establish that every MBA or business-school fee is covered. Record admission fees and any uncovered tuition before applying.

For PhD funding, identify the research area, supervisor or advertised project and whether admission includes a funding decision. A research placement or fellowship is not automatically a degree scholarship.

Documents Pakistani students should organise early

Use the name and date of birth shown on your passport consistently. Keep clear degree and transcript scans, an official grading-scale explanation, employment evidence where required, referee contact details and certified translations for any document not accepted in its original language.

Attestation, equivalence, nomination and testing rules are programme-specific. HEC, IBCC, a university, an embassy or another authority may be involved depending on the qualification and route, but no single checklist applies to every scholarship. Follow the current official call and each portal's document instructions.

  • Valid passport or accepted identity document
  • Degrees, transcripts and result evidence
  • Institutional grading scale or CGPA explanation
  • Employment letters with dates and duties
  • Language evidence accepted by the chosen course
  • References submitted through the required channel
  • Research proposal for relevant doctoral routes

English-language requirements are separate decisions

A scholarship may not impose one universal IELTS score while the university still requires language evidence. Check the scholarship, selected course and visa process separately. Possible alternatives such as TOEFL, PTE, a Medium of Instruction letter or an institutional assessment are accepted only when the responsible institution says so for your intake.

Do not rely on a headline promising a scholarship without IELTS. Obtain written confirmation and consider whether one recognised test would keep more strong-fit programmes open across your full shortlist.

Important CERN eligibility warning for Pakistani nationals

CERN's current Short-Term Internship and Origin Programme pages state that candidates of several nationalities, including Pakistani nationals, cannot currently be considered because the applicable nationality ceiling has been reached. This is a current programme condition, not a judgement about an applicant's qualifications.

Rules can change, so reopen the official CERN page before a future application. Do not pay an intermediary who claims to bypass a nationality or membership condition, and do not present CERN as currently available to Pakistani applicants when the programme page says otherwise.

How to write a Pakistan-to-programme fit argument

Avoid a generic promise to help Pakistan. Define a problem you understand through study, work or service, show what you have already done, name the skill or network gap the programme addresses and describe a realistic next step after the award.

A credible plan may focus on a sector, institution, research question, community or professional practice. Use evidence at the scale you can support. Scholarship selectors can distinguish a thoughtful sequence from an oversized impact claim written only for the application.

Avoid agents, copied essays and false guarantees

Major scholarship programmes publish their official routes. Nobody can guarantee selection, and an applicant remains responsible for truthful documents and original responses. Verify every fee on the programme or university domain, never send money to a personal account and do not give an agent access to your email or one-time codes.

Choose the correct programme and country route

Nationality is one eligibility condition, not a complete decision. FLEX Global directs Pakistani applicants to the country office, while degree scholarships and research placements use different academic requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Are Chevening and Commonwealth applications currently open for Pakistan?+

At the 25 October 2026 check, Chevening Pakistan and Commonwealth Master’s 2027–2028 applications are closed. Follow the current official page for amendments or a future call; later selection tasks do not reopen the scholarship form.

Which scholarship route should an undergraduate applicant use?+

Start with awards that explicitly fund first degrees and check entry-year and nomination conditions. Master’s and PhD scholarship rules cannot be applied to an undergraduate application.

Can a master’s scholarship pay for an MBA?+

Only when that award and institution permit the exact MBA and cover the relevant fees. Check eligible-course rules and exclusions instead of treating all master’s degrees as interchangeable.

Can I get a scholarship abroad without IELTS from Pakistan?+

Sometimes, if the scholarship and chosen institution accept another test, Medium of Instruction evidence or a waiver. Admission, scholarship and visa rules must be checked separately in writing.

Does HEC nominate applicants for every scholarship?+

No. Some routes involve HEC or another national body, while others use an embassy, university, consortium or direct programme portal. Follow the exact current process for each award.
