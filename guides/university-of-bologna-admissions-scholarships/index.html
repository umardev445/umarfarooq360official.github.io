University of Bologna: admissions, fees and ER.GO funding

Bologna admission starts with the selected degree, not one university-wide deadline. Use this guide to separate academic selection, fee exemptions and regional benefits.

Updated 13 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

The short answer

Find the exact degree and its call, check your applicant category and follow the stated application route. ER.GO support and university fee exemptions are separate from admission.

Next step: Choose a bachelor's or master's programme and note its campus, teaching language, current call and portal.

Find the programme and campus that fit

The degree catalogue separates first/single-cycle and second-cycle programmes. Open the degree's own website for entry requirements, selection process and language conditions. The catalogue observed during this review shows 2026–2027 offerings; do not assume it confirms future 2027–2028 deadlines.

Record the campus before comparing living costs. A programme attached to the University of Bologna is not necessarily taught in central Bologna. Compare the curriculum, where you will attend classes and what practical or project work the course includes.

Admission and English requirements

Bologna's non-EU master's route distinguishes open and restricted access and directs applicants to the programme notice for English or other language evidence. No single IELTS/PTE score is assigned to the whole university here.

Where the programme uses Studenti Online, create the correct university credentials and follow its admission instructions. Some degrees use different tests or selection steps. Do not assume creating an account is the same as completing an application.

Fees: fixed charge plus programme contribution

For 2026–2027, Bologna publishes a fixed €157.04 component plus a variable contribution determined by the programme and economic assessment. Without the required evidence or eligibility for exemption, the full programme amount can apply.

Read the fee page for your year of study and category. Do not compare the fixed charge with another university's full annual contribution. Confirm what remains payable after a waiver and whether application costs are separate.

Fee exemptions and ER.GO scholarships are not the same deadline

Bologna's current fee-exemption instructions list 30 October 2026 at 18:00 for personal/economic data, a late route to 16 November at 18:00 with a €100 fee and foreign-document upload by 20 November. These dates concern 2026–2027 fee exemptions, not a new ER.GO scholarship deadline or 2027–2028 admission.

Organise overseas financial evidence

Where income or assets are held abroad, use the institution's foreign-document route. Do not assume a CAF certificate prepared for Padua replaces ER.GO's required evidence.

List each household member and document category before arranging translation. Check the reference year and official issuer, then retain the original and submitted version together. Use the ISEE guide for the comparison of assessment routes, and the current Bologna/ER.GO instructions for your actual application.

After admission: pre-enrolment, originals and housing

Follow the applicable international-student route for Universitaly and qualification verification. Ask whether your admission remains conditional and what must be completed before matriculation. Keep the academic offer and visa documents aligned.

Start a housing shortlist early but verify each offer. Record the location, commute, deposit, included utilities and cancellation rules. A benefit application or a place on a ranking is not a reason to assume that a room is already allocated.

A practical next-step checklist

Before you commit, your notes should identify one programme, one admission route, one realistic fee category and one plan for living costs. Missing information belongs on a question list for the official office, not in your budget as a guessed zero.

  • Programme call and applicant category saved.
  • Teaching language and evidence confirmed.
  • Application portal and selection steps understood.
  • Fee-exemption and benefit dates kept separate.
  • Current overseas-document instructions checked.
  • Campus-specific housing and arrival costs budgeted.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bologna's admission deadline?+

The exact programme call determines the deadline and applicant category. There is no single date valid for every degree.

Is Bologna tuition only €157.04?+

That is the published fixed component for 2026–2027. A variable programme contribution can also apply unless the relevant exemption is granted.

Does a fee exemption include an ER.GO scholarship?+

Not automatically. Scholarships, accommodation and fee support have distinct conditions and application tasks.
