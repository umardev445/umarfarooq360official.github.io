The short answer

UCAS points are added from the tariff value of each included qualification. This calculator supports GCE A/AS levels, three named BTEC National sizes, EPQ and full T Levels; it does not decide whether a university accepts them.

Next step: Read your exact qualification title and offer conditions before choosing inputs. A points total does not replace a required subject or grade.

What this calculator supports

Choose the qualification name that appears on your results record. The word Diploma alone is not enough to identify a BTEC tariff: different sizes have different grade scales. A GCE A level and an International A level should not be assumed to share every lookup entry; use the official calculator for an unlisted title.

Follow the qualification-to-offer workflow

First enter your results as awarded or predicted. Then remove qualifications your chosen course does not count. Finally compare the remaining total with the complete offer, including subjects, units and separate GCSE requirements. The same academic record can therefore produce a general tariff total and a smaller course-counted total.

Worked example

B in Biology (40), B in Chemistry (40) and C in Mathematics (32) give 112 points. If a course also requires B in Mathematics, this combination does not satisfy that named-grade condition despite reaching 112.

Avoid double counting overlapping study

This tool recognises an AS entered alongside an A level with the same subject label, ignoring case and surrounding spaces. It excludes that AS from the estimate. Without matching labels, the tool cannot infer that the study overlaps. It also cannot decide whether two vocational qualifications contain duplicated units.

UCAS explains that providers can restrict accepted qualifications and generally count the highest achievement in overlapping study. Use the include checkbox to model the course rule, but obtain confirmation if the overlap is unclear.

Read the reverse lookup correctly

The reverse lookup lists combinations of three passing GCE A levels. It returns exact totals where possible; otherwise it displays the smallest available total at or above the target. It does not round a target into a guaranteed offer match.

For example, a target of 113 has no exact three-passing-A-level match under these values. The next total is 120. This is useful arithmetic for a minimum-points condition, but it cannot substitute for an offer specifying particular grades.

Check a qualification outside the supported list

Scottish qualifications, Access to HE Diplomas, IB components, partial T Levels and other vocational awards can require different tables or additional information. Omitting them from this simplified calculator does not mean that they lack tariff points or are unacceptable to a university. Use the official UCAS lookup for the exact title and application cycle.

Frequently asked questions

Does the total prove I meet my offer?+

No. A course can restrict the qualifications it counts or require particular subjects, grades and other conditions.

Can I include an EPQ?+

Yes, the calculator includes EPQ tariff values. Exclude it if your course does not count it towards the offer.

Why is my AS worth zero in the breakdown?+

An included A level has the same subject label, so the overlapping AS is excluded. It is not a statement that every AS grade is worth zero.

Is this an official UCAS service?+

No. AspireCompass is an independent educational site. Confirm qualification values and acceptance with UCAS and the university.
