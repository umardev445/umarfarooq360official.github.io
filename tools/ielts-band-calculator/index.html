The short answer

IELTS overall band is the average of the four section bands, rounded to the nearest half band. An overall target and a minimum in each section are different conditions.

Overall score versus minimum section score

A course may require an overall band and a separate minimum in every component. An overall 7.0 does not automatically satisfy a requirement of no section below 6.5 if one component is 6.0.

Record the exact university, degree, intake, overall requirement and component minimum. Scholarship and admission language conditions can be separate, and immigration evidence may involve another check.

Listening and Reading raw marks

IELTS Listening and Reading each contain 40 questions. Official guidance notes that raw marks are converted to band scores and that the precise marks needed can vary slightly between test versions. Academic and General Training Reading also use different typical mark patterns.

For that reason, this tool asks for section bands rather than claiming that one unofficial raw-mark table can predict every test form. Use the official IELTS score explanation for current indicative marks and your official report for application evidence.

How to use the calculator for scholarship planning

First compare the estimated overall and lowest section with the exact course condition. Next identify the weakest component and plan practice around the marking criteria rather than repeatedly taking full tests without diagnosis.

If a scholarship advertises an IELTS waiver, check the university rule independently. Medium of Instruction letters and alternative tests are institution-specific routes, not universal replacements.

Frequently asked questions

How is the IELTS overall band calculated?+

Add Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, divide by four and round the average to the nearest half band under the published IELTS rule.

What happens when the average is 6.25?+

The official IELTS scoring explanation shows that an average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band, so 6.25 becomes 6.5.

What happens when the average is 6.75?+

An average ending in .75 rounds up to the next whole band, so 6.75 becomes 7.0.

Is meeting the overall band enough for university admission?+

Not always. Many courses also require a minimum score in each section. Follow the exact course and intake requirement.

Can I submit this calculator result with an application?+

No. It is an estimate for planning. Submit only the official language evidence accepted by the programme or institution.
