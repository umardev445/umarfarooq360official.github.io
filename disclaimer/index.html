Not an official programme representative

AspireCompass is not affiliated with, endorsed by or authorised to speak for the scholarships, institutions, governments, employers or organisations it describes unless a page explicitly states otherwise. Programme names are used for identification and research.

Information can change

We work to present useful summaries and checked dates, but application windows, eligibility, funding, benefits, links and procedures can change after review. An error, omission or delayed update is possible. The programme owner's current publication and direct communication are final.

No admission, funding or employment guarantee

Eligibility does not guarantee selection, and a strong application does not create a right to an award. AspireCompass cannot predict or influence admissions, scholarships, interviews, jobs, fellowships, exchange nominations or immigration decisions.

Funding labels need verification

Terms such as fully funded, funded and paid summarise a programme's published description. Benefits may exclude tests, document preparation, dependants, deposits, visa-related charges, insurance, travel or costs above a stipend. Build a personal budget from the official award terms.

No legal, immigration, financial or medical advice

Country, visa, work-right, tax, cost and safety discussion is general. Seek current information from the relevant authority and a qualified professional when your situation requires personalised advice. Do not make a financial commitment solely from an AspireCompass page.

Examples and application guidance

Writing examples are illustrative and must not be copied as personal work. Applicants are responsible for truthfulness, originality, privacy and compliance with each institution's rules, including any limits on AI or third-party assistance.

External websites and advertising

AspireCompass does not control external content or transactions. Advertising, if displayed, helps support the website but does not convert an advertiser into an endorsed opportunity or give it influence over editorial conclusions.

Frequently asked questions

Is an AspireCompass deadline final?+

No. It records what was found at the checked date. Confirm the deadline, time zone and any earlier nomination date on the official current page.

Does fully funded mean I will pay nothing?+

Not necessarily. Read the exact benefits and exclusions and estimate costs that occur before payment or above the allowance.

Can AspireCompass decide if I am eligible?+

No. Pages can help you compare published rules, but only the responsible programme can give an authoritative decision about a specific application.

Are displayed ads recommendations?+

No. Advertising is separate from editorial research and should not be interpreted as a recommendation or verification.
