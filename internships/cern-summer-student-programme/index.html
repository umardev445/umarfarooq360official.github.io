CERN Summer Student Programme: choose a route and build an evidence file

Choose the programme that fits your work and study stage, then prepare evidence you can explain. This guide connects the current CERN vacancies with an original route comparison and practical preparation worksheet.

Updated 15 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

At the 15 November 2026 review, CERN’s standard and openlab 2027 vacancy pages offered applications and listed 21 January 2027, 23:59 Geneva time. They are distinct research routes; choose one using the exact vacancy and your evidence, then recheck availability before applying.

Next step: Record the job reference, verify the study/enrolment conditions and complete a project-fit and document audit before submitting.

Start with the 2027 vacancy, not the year inside a web address

The standard listing is identified as SUMM-2027 and gives an ideal start of 14 June 2027; the openlab listing is SUM-OPENLAB-2027 with an ideal start of 21 June 2027. Both list the same closing instant at this review. The pages’ web addresses retain an older year, so use the body’s programme title, reference and current application link together.

For the listed January deadline, Geneva is on CET, UTC+1: 23:59 Geneva corresponds to 22:59 UTC on 21 January 2027. Write both the owner’s time zone and your own local equivalent in your private plan. Leave enough time for document or connection problems; opening a form near the cutoff is not a completed application.

Save the exact vacancy reference beside your draft. A search snippet or an archived guide is weaker evidence than the current owner listing. If the body, linked form or offer does not match the cycle you intend, resolve that mismatch before entering personal information.

Compare the research task before choosing the programme

CERN’s general Summer Studentship overview describes scientific or technical project work and an eight-to-thirteen-week association. The openlab programme describes a nine-week computing-focused placement. Its programme page lists 21 or 28 June 2027 starts. Vacancy summary cards also use a two-month contract label; confirm your actual approved dates and duration in the final offer rather than deriving them from that card alone.

A computing student can see plausible work in both routes. That does not make the applications interchangeable. Read the department/project description, identify the methods you can contribute and state what you want to learn. A familiar programme name is not evidence of a project match.

Check the study conditions without treating this guide as an eligibility result

The current vacancies specify bachelor’s/master’s study rather than PhD candidature, at least six completed full-time semesters by the summer start, continued full-time enrolment during the placement, English ability and restrictions on previous CERN participation. The exact field requirement and full conditions belong to the selected vacancy. Check each one rather than assuming that meeting the semester condition is sufficient.

Make a private yes/uncertain/no record against the actual wording. For example, a final-year applicant should establish whether they remain enrolled during their intended placement; an academic transcript alone may not answer that. An uncertain condition needs clarification or a different route, not an invented interpretation in the form.

Do not send passport numbers or full academic files to an unofficial adviser merely to ask a general eligibility question. The programme owner supplies the authoritative submission channel and decides how your record is assessed.

Build an original project-fit worksheet from work you actually did

This worksheet is an AspireCompass preparation exercise, not a CERN selection rubric or a model application to copy. Write one concrete task, your individual contribution, the evidence that exists and the method you hope to learn. Replace broad claims such as “I am expert in AI” with a small, accurate account that a referee could recognise.

Fictional example: a student helped analyse instrument readings in a course project. They removed invalid rows, wrote reproducible checks and compared a baseline model with a simple alternative. They did not design the instrument, lead a laboratory or publish a paper. Their research interest can build on the genuine analysis task without converting it into achievements they do not have.

Read a current project or department description and identify the connection in your own words. If you cannot explain that connection, revise the shortlist. Keep the learning gap honest: needing supervised training is more informative than pretending to know a method from its name.

Finish the document dependencies before the final form

The standard vacancy asks for a CV, academic reference, enrolment certificate and official transcripts in English or French. The openlab programme page also specifies labelled PDF files, a recent academic reference and an optional motivation letter. Its instructions say requested documents cannot be added after the application is completed. Read the selected route’s live requirements before using any generic checklist.

Start with items controlled by other people. Ask the appropriate academic contact about a genuine reference and obtain the institution’s official enrolment record. Then audit file type, readability, cycle, identity consistency and the permitted language. A folder named “final” is not proof that every file meets the live form’s requirements.

Use separate states for requested, received, inspected and submitted. A reference request is not a received letter, and a downloaded transcript that will not open is not ready to upload. Keep personal documents private and use the owner’s application route for submission.

Budget the allowance separately from accommodation and payment timing

The current listings state CHF 94 per calendar day and health-insurance coverage, with a stated exception for some nationally funded students. They describe travel support as conditional and accommodation help as assistance finding housing. That is not an assurance of a free room. The openlab programme describes its travel allowance as a lump sum at the end of the stay; confirm your own final arrangements.

Original arithmetic exercise: nine weeks contain 63 calendar days, and 63 × CHF 94 is CHF 5,922. This multiplication is not an award quotation. Your contract dates, applicable funding arrangement and payment conditions determine the actual entitlement. Do not apply the same total to a placement with a different duration.

Create a cash-timing plan as well as a total budget. Suppose an invented housing quote requires a CHF 900 deposit and CHF 1,100 before arrival: the immediate cash need is CHF 2,000 before travel and other costs. A later reimbursement cannot pay that earlier bill. Replace these fictional numbers with verified arrangements and distinguish a refundable deposit from an expense.

Run a final consistency check before submission

Open your chosen vacancy again and compare its reference with the form you are completing. Check the intended period against enrolment, your claimed skills against your actual contribution, and each file against the live instruction. Correct a mismatch before it becomes part of the submitted record.

Read every answer as a reviewer who has not seen your private notes. Would they understand what you did, why this route fits and what evidence supports it? Remove unsupported claims and keep the application in your own voice. An original worksheet should help you think clearly, not manufacture a background or a ready-made statement.

After submission, retain the owner’s confirmation and the final files in a private folder. Continue checking the official channel for any legitimate follow-up. The preparation checklist can establish that you have organised your tasks; it cannot predict selection, assign a project or replace CERN’s decision.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a standard summer application as an openlab application?+

Treat them as separate vacancies. Compare the exact job reference and form; do not assume one submission enters both routes.

Does the listed allowance mean housing is free?+

No. The listings distinguish an allowance from help finding accommodation. Confirm the actual housing and travel arrangements before making a budget.

Is the project-fit example a successful CERN application?+

No. It is an original fictional preparation exercise. Use your own real contribution and the current programme instructions.
