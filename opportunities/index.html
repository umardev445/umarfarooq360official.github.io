Frequently asked questions

How do I know whether an opportunity shown here is still open?+

Use the status and deadline as a screening signal, then open the linked programme-owner page before preparing or submitting an application. The official call is the final authority.

Why are scholarships, internships and fellowships separated?+

They use different eligibility and selection models. Filtering by opportunity type prevents a student scholarship requirement from being confused with an employer or fellowship requirement.

What should I record before adding an opportunity to my shortlist?+

Record the official owner, applicant eligibility, degree or career level, deadline with time zone, funding or pay, application route and any separate university or nomination step.
