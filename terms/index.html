Informational service only

AspireCompass organises public opportunity information and publishes general educational guidance. It is not a scholarship provider, university, recruiter, government body, immigration adviser, legal adviser or application agent. Use the service as a research starting point and verify decisions with the responsible organisation.

No guarantee of availability or outcome

A listing does not guarantee that an opportunity is open, suitable, safe, fully funded or available to a particular person. Programme owners can change rules without notice. AspireCompass does not guarantee admission, funding, interviews, employment, visas, response times or error-free access.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for checking current official eligibility, deadlines, time zones, costs, document rules and application routes. You are also responsible for the accuracy and legality of material you submit to an external programme.

  • Use the official programme portal

  • Do not misrepresent qualifications or experience
  • Keep independent copies of applications and receipts

Can I republish AspireCompass listings on my own website?+

You may link to pages and quote a limited extract with attribution, but may not copy or resell substantial parts of the database or original guides without permission.

No. External organisations control their portals, decisions, security and terms. Verify the domain and read their policies before submitting information.

No. Website use and contact correspondence do not create legal, immigration, educational, recruitment or financial advisory services.
