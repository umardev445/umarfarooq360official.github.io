Information and accuracy

Listings are summaries, not legal or official advice. Details can change after our last check. You are responsible for reading the current official call and meeting its requirements before applying.

Privacy and advertising

The site does not require a reader account or ask users to upload passports, financial records or academic documents. The dedicated Privacy Policy explains technical logs, contact emails, cookies, Google advertising and consent controls where required.

External links

External websites have their own privacy, security and application practices. A link does not imply control or endorsement. Check the domain carefully before uploading personal documents.

Opportunity safety checklist

Government, university and established employer programmes normally publish on recognisable official domains and use documented selection processes.

  • Never pay for a guaranteed scholarship or job
  • Do not send money to a personal account
  • Check email domains and portal URLs
  • Do not share passwords or one-time codes
  • Report suspicious copies to the programme owner

Acceptable use and copyright

You may use AspireCompass for personal opportunity research. Do not scrape, republish or sell substantial portions of the database or misrepresent AspireCompass content as official programme material. Programme names belong to their respective owners.

Policy documents

Use the related-page links below to read the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Disclaimer and Editorial Policy. They explain data handling, advertising, acceptable use, sourcing, corrections and the limits of the information on this website.

A five-minute opportunity verification

Before sharing documents, open the programme through its official organisation domain, confirm the current intake and search the organisation's own site for the opportunity name. Compare the contact email, application portal and fee information with the official page.

  • Check the full domain character by character
  • Confirm the named intake and deadline
  • Read eligibility on the programme owner's page
  • Verify any fee with the university or programme
  • Save the submission confirmation

Red flags that require a pause

Stop when a contact promises a guaranteed award, demands urgent payment to a personal account, asks for passwords or one-time codes, or avoids providing a verifiable programme page. Report an impersonation to the organisation being copied and the relevant platform or authority.

Frequently asked questions

Does an AspireCompass link mean a programme is guaranteed?+

No. A link helps you reach a source; it is not a guarantee of eligibility, availability, safety or selection. Complete your own current checks before applying.

Does AspireCompass charge an application fee?+

No. AspireCompass does not accept applications or charge applicants. A university or third-party test provider may have its own legitimate fees, which you must verify independently.

Where can I read the advertising privacy information?+

Read the separate Privacy Policy. It explains cookies, Google advertising, consent controls and user choices that apply when advertising services are active.
