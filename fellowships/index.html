Match the fellowship to your stage

Some fellowships are designed for enrolled students, others for professionals with several years of impact. Review the cohort profile and previous fellows to understand the real level of competitiveness.

  • Career-stage requirement
  • Residency and travel expectations
  • Project or research proposal
  • Employer approval or nomination
  • Post-fellowship commitments

Read the commitment behind the funding

A fellowship may involve residential sessions, travel, a project, employer approval, public events or a return-home commitment. Put every required date beside your study and work calendar before applying.

Look beyond the stipend. Mentoring quality, cohort access, project ownership and alumni support often determine whether a fellowship creates lasting value.

Build a convincing fellowship proposal

Describe a defined problem, the people affected, the approach you can realistically take during the fellowship and the evidence you will use to judge progress. Avoid promising national-scale change from a short programme.

Prepare evidence for fellowship selection

Build a short evidence bank showing responsibility, outcomes, collaboration and reflection. Connect each example to the fellowship's stated purpose instead of submitting one general leadership story everywhere.

Confirm whether references, employer permission, a host invitation or a project budget must be ready before the deadline.

Choose by career stage, purpose and commitment

Compare the qualification or experience required, the activity funded, the support period and any residency or reporting obligation. The label fellowship does not establish an employment contract or complete funding.

Frequently asked questions

Is a fellowship the same as a scholarship?+

Not always. Scholarships usually support study, while fellowships may support research, work placements, leadership programmes or projects. Read the activity and eligibility rules.

Can students apply for professional fellowships?+

Only when the programme permits their career stage. Many fellowships require graduation, work experience or evidence of professional impact.
