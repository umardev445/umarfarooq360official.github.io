CAS-ANSO scholarship: USTC, UCAS and the application route

The name ANSO appears in several research programmes. This guide concerns the CAS-ANSO degree scholarship and explains how to choose the correct postgraduate host route.

Updated 22 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

The published CAS-ANSO degree programme supports master’s and doctoral study through USTC, UCAS and relevant CAS institutions. Use the current call and host instructions together; do not assume a new cycle is open because an older call remains online.

Next step: Choose the degree and host route, read the current ANSO eligibility conditions, then match a suitable programme or research group.

Published call and current application status

The official call reviewed is labelled the 2026 CAS-ANSO degree scholarship and was published on 15 October 2025. It is an earlier-cycle reference, not proof that a 2027 round is accepting applications. Check the ANSO programme page and the host’s newest notice for a later call.

If you are preparing before a new call appears, work on academic fit, transcripts and research planning without claiming an unannounced deadline. Already selected candidates should follow the host’s award and arrival instructions rather than an old application article.

USTC, UCAS and CAS: choose a route, not just a name

The official degree call identifies USTC and UCAS, with CAS institutes participating through the relevant host arrangements. Choose a programme and supervisor route that matches your field, rather than selecting an institution solely because it appears in a scholarship list.

Admission requirements and application systems are set by the host. Read the host guide alongside the ANSO conditions. A CAS research group’s interest does not remove the need to complete formal university admission and funding steps.

Eligibility is a set of conditions, not just a good GPA

Compare your nationality, previous qualification, age or date-of-birth condition, language evidence and other restrictions against the current call. Use the actual wording and cutoff date; do not carry a previous year’s age threshold into a new intake without checking.

If you are already studying in China or applying under another award, read any restrictions and compatibility clauses carefully. Prepare a list of unclear conditions and ask the responsible host office using your exact situation. Do not ask an agent to guess eligibility from a GPA alone.

Research fit and the proposal

A useful proposal explains the problem, why it matters, the method and why the host is suitable. Show that you understand the group’s actual work without copying its website into your statement. Keep the scope realistic for your intended degree.

For supervisor contact, use the host’s instructions. Introduce your academic preparation, a concise research interest and the relevant attachment if requested. An unanswered email is not an official rejection, while a positive reply is not a scholarship award.

Funding and what you should verify in writing

Read the package under the exact call and host route. Separate tuition support, stipend, insurance, travel and accommodation rather than applying the phrase “fully funded” to every possible cost. Check the award duration and the academic review needed for continuation.

Housing is particularly easy to misunderstand: a stipend or tuition waiver does not establish that a residence room is free. Confirm housing charges, availability and deductions with the host and award letter before calculating the remaining budget.

Submission and later decisions

Follow both the scholarship and host instructions for the systems and selections required. Save final submission records and the research materials you sent. If the host requests an interview or additional evidence, use the official channel and respond within the stated time.

Do not accept a paid promise of selection. Final admission, scholarship decisions and visa assessment belong to different authorities. After an award, read the host’s enrolment and arrival conditions before making non-refundable arrangements.

Frequently asked questions

Is ANSO only for PhD students?+

The published degree call includes master’s and doctoral study. Check the current host and degree conditions.

Is the next application round confirmed open?+

This guide does not confirm a 2027 opening. The cited official degree call is for 2026; check newer official announcements.

Can I assume housing is free?+

No. Read the precise funding package and the host’s accommodation conditions before budgeting.
