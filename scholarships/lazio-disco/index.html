Lazio DiSCo scholarship: eligibility, documents and application

Applying to a university in Lazio? Use this guide to organise the regional scholarship application, financial evidence and housing request without mixing them with your university admission.

Updated 22 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

DiSCo is the regional right-to-study route for eligible institutions in Lazio. Admission alone does not enter you in the scholarship competition, and a scholarship application does not secure a university place.

Next step: Open the current DiSCo call, find your institution and applicant category, then record every application stage and document deadline.

Current cycle: do not confuse a completion deadline with a new application

The official 2026/27 notice published on 7 August stated that 11 August 2026 at 12:00 was the second-phase deadline for applicants admitted to that phase. That date has passed. It was not a fresh application opening for somebody who had not completed the earlier stage.

For a future intake, start at the current call and its amendments. Do not recycle dates from the previous competition. For an already submitted application, use the reserved area to check requests, rankings and any corrections that are still permitted.

Official reference: DiSCo 2026/27 second-phase notice

Check the institution and funding route before collecting documents

Use the list of eligible institutions in the call, not the city in an advertisement. A student in Rome should verify the actual awarding institution and course. Sapienza admission and Lazio DiSCo are separate procedures with separate accounts and responsibilities.

Then establish your degree level, year of study and whether this is a first application or a renewal. Keep a copy of the exact category you selected. Academic progress and previous qualifications can affect eligibility even when family income is low.

Prepare foreign-income evidence as a consistent file

Start with the household definition and reference period stated in the call. Make a document map for family composition, income, assets and any circumstances requiring a specific certificate. Each document should identify the person, issuing authority, period and currency where relevant.

Translation and legalisation depend on the current rules and country of issue. Do not assume that an English bank balance replaces a required income or property certificate. If a document cannot be issued in your country, ask DiSCo about the accepted alternative before paying for a substitute.

Housing status and cash support are different decisions

Read the resident, commuter and away-from-home definitions before estimating support. A housing request, a private rental contract and an assigned residence place are different types of evidence. Do not budget as if a room is guaranteed when you have only requested accommodation.

Build two budgets: one with the benefit actually confirmed in writing and one with no early payment. Include rent deposit, travel and the first weeks of living expenses. This protects your plan if the ranking, accommodation allocation or payment takes longer than your arrival schedule.

Worked example

Planning example: if you must arrive before a payment date, list the rent deposit and first month separately from your later annual budget. An expected award is not money already available in your account.

Submit, inspect and retain a receipt

Work through the application using the official instructions for your identity and residence situation. Review every field before the final submission step. Saving a draft is not the same as submitting an application.

After submitting, check the portal rather than relying only on email. Save the application number, final version, attachments and timestamps. If a ranking shows an exclusion, read the stated reason and correction or appeal route; a general message to a university may not reach the authority handling the benefit.

Renewal and competing awards

Winning once does not remove the need to check the next competition. Record credit requirements, the date by which credits must be earned, enrolment conditions and any incompatibility with another award. A change from bachelor’s to master’s studies can require a different application category.

Before accepting several benefits, compare the compatibility clauses. Ask the awarding bodies about the combination in writing and keep their answer with your records. Avoid committing the same expense to two grants when their rules prohibit it.

Frequently asked questions

Is DiSCo the same as a Sapienza scholarship?+

No. DiSCo is a regional benefit route. Sapienza admission and any university-specific award have their own procedures.

Can I still use the August 2026 deadline?+

That published second-phase deadline has passed. Check current official notices for your application and intake; it does not establish a new round.

Does being eligible guarantee a room?+

Do not assume so. Eligibility, award status and accommodation allocation must be read separately in the current call and ranking.
