Gates Cambridge Scholarship 2027: deadlines and application

For Gates Cambridge 2027/28, the US-resident US-citizen round has closed. Other eligible applicants use the funding deadline of their chosen course: 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027. Prepare the course application, additional scholarship answers and references together.

Updated 3 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Use the selected course's funding deadline: 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027 at 23:59 GMT for the main funding round. The January date is not a second chance for a course with a December deadline. The US-resident US-citizen round closed on 14 October.

Gates Cambridge 2027: choose the correct round

The 2027/28 scholarship is tied to an eligible postgraduate course application. For applicants outside the US-resident US-citizen round, use the exact course funding deadline. An admission deadline later in the year is not the scholarship deadline.

Check course eligibility before writing the scholarship answers

Use the Cambridge funding search entry for the award year and your intended course. The official international record describes master's and PhD funding for eligible applicants outside the UK and directs US citizens resident in the US to their separate round. Course mode and excluded qualifications require particular attention; a general postgraduate label does not make every course eligible.

Save the course title, code, start date and funding deadline in one place. For research degrees, follow the department's instructions on supervisor contact and research proposals. Being interested in Cambridge is not a substitute for explaining why the specific academic route fits your preparation.

Official reference: Cambridge: Gates international award record for 2027/28

Match your evidence to the selection criteria

The official award record names academic excellence, reasons for the course, commitment to improving lives and leadership capacity. Build a small evidence bank before drafting. For each criterion, identify an actual event, your contribution and what you learned; avoid repeating the same broad claim in every answer.

The Applicant Portal controls the current questions and limits. Read those fields directly and preserve your own voice. A reference should help assess the scholarship criteria using evidence known to the referee, rather than merely repeat a transcript.

Application and reference sequence

Cambridge requires a complete application, including references, by the relevant funding deadline. Create an internal follow-up date for each referee. Verify any Gates-specific reference request in the current funding section rather than assuming that academic references alone finish the task.

  1. 1Select an eligible course and save its funding deadline.
  2. 2Open the course application and follow the funding section's Gates instructions.
  3. 3Invite the required referees and agree a submission plan.
  4. 4Prepare the course documents and additional scholarship answers.
  5. 5Check the complete portal record, submit before the funding deadline and save confirmation.

Worked example

A fictional course has an 8 December funding deadline and a later admission closing date. Submitting on 6 January does not create a second chance for that course's funding. Record 8 December as the controlling date and work backwards from it.

Funding scope and costs to verify

Cambridge describes the Gates award as full-cost support covering the composition fee and maintenance, with travel, visa and immigration-health support. Some additional components are discretionary. Use the current award terms and your offer rather than treating a headline package valuation as a cash payment.

Make a timing budget as well as an annual budget. Identify payments needed before departure and when support becomes available. For research costs, dependants or a change of course, ask the appropriate office which rules apply and keep its response with your plan.

Before submitting for Gates consideration

Review the full course and funding application together. A form marked submitted can still have an outstanding reference or a required document that affects its completeness.

Check the submission, then save the evidence

A saved draft and a submitted application are different states. Reopen the application summary, inspect every attachment and check the referee indicators. Keep the confirmation email or reference number with the version of the instructions you used. Put any later transcript, offer-acceptance or enrolment task in a separate calendar entry.

The tracker is an editable planning sheet, not an application form or eligibility assessment. Its suggested next actions belong to you; deadlines and requirements remain controlled by the institution. Replace the blank owner, internal deadline and confirmation fields with your own plan.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose either the December or January Gates deadline?+

No. The applicable course funding deadline controls your application. Check the course page; a later admission closing date does not extend funding consideration.

Do references have to be in by the funding deadline?+

Cambridge states that the complete application, including references, must arrive by 23:59 GMT on the relevant deadline. Check any Gates-specific reference request as well as course references.

Can Pakistani applicants use the main round?+

Eligible applicants outside the US-resident US-citizen category use the international route. Nationality alone does not establish eligibility for the selected course or award.
