Start with eligibility, not prestige

A famous scholarship is useful only when your citizenship, degree level, field, work experience and intended course satisfy the published rules. Build a shortlist in three tiers: strong fit, possible fit and aspirational.

For every shortlisted award, record its funding scope. Fully funded can still exclude dependants, visa charges, tests or some travel costs.

  • Confirm nationality and residency rules
  • Check degree and graduation-date requirements
  • Identify required work or leadership experience
  • Verify whether university admission is separate
  • Calculate costs not covered by the award

Build a scholarship portfolio, not one risky bet

Divide your shortlist into opportunities with different selection models. A government leadership scholarship, a university merit award and a research assistantship may judge different strengths. This reduces dependence on one result and helps you reuse verified evidence without sending identical applications.

Record the official owner, intake, application route, funding exclusions and decision schedule for every programme. Remove an opportunity when a mandatory rule fails; time spent improving a strong-fit application is more valuable than trying to argue around an eligibility condition.

Choose a path by degree before opening a form

Undergraduate applicants may need early school nomination, as with the Pearson award. Master's candidates should decide whether the scholarship and admission are separate, as with Chevening, or handled through a course consortium, as with Erasmus Mundus. Doctoral applicants should identify research fit and any supervisor or partner-nomination requirement before drafting a proposal.

Choose your country, qualification and realistic budget first. Use the linked country guides for local eligibility details, then open the programme owner's current call before adding an award to your shortlist.

  • Bachelor's: start with school records and nomination
  • Master's: map scholarship and university deadlines
  • PhD: confirm research and supervisor process
  • All levels: verify funding exclusions and language proof

Research Italy through three funding owners

For Italy, compare the MAECI government call, the competent regional DSU authority and university-specific merit or tuition support. Each can have its own portal, documents and deadline.

The dedicated Italy scholarship page labels closed 2026–2027 calls clearly and links to the official source, so applicants can plan for the next cycle without copying an expired date.
