Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme 2027/28: application guide

The Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme supports new full-time doctoral researchers at eight participating universities. A successful application plan has two parts: obtain the RGC reference number, then complete the selected universities' own PhD admission applications.

Updated 3 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

The HKPFS initial application closes on 1 December 2026 at 12:00 noon Hong Kong time (UTC+8). Obtain the reference number and quote it in separate university applications. You may choose up to two programmes at one or two participating universities.

Next step: Check the selected departments, shortlist the two choices and record each university's own requirements and deadline before submitting the initial form.

Eligibility and the eight participating universities

The scheme is for new full-time PhD applicants, irrespective of country of origin. RGC considers academic excellence, research potential, communication and leadership. The chosen university still decides its academic, language and departmental admission requirements.

  • City University of Hong Kong
  • Hong Kong Baptist University
  • Lingnan University
  • The Chinese University of Hong Kong
  • The Education University of Hong Kong
  • The Hong Kong Polytechnic University
  • The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology
  • The University of Hong Kong

Funding: distinguish the scheme from university additions

RGC announces 400 fellowships for 2027/28. Its overview still labels the HK$344,400 annual stipend and HK$14,400 annual research-travel allowance as 2026/27 figures. HKU's explicitly labelled 2027/28 page confirms HK$28,700 monthly and HK$14,400 annual travel support for its awardees. Read the current university package rather than silently changing the year on a figure.

HKU also publishes tuition support and fourth-year arrangements for its HKPFS holders. Those are HKU conditions, not automatic benefits at all eight institutions. For another university, check fees, additional years, housing and any top-up separately. Record whether support is a stipend, a fee waiver or a reimbursement.

Build the two-stage application plan

RGC's application page currently lists university deadlines later on 1 December, but departments and document processes can introduce earlier practical constraints. The noon initial deadline must be met even if a university form closes later that night. Do not treat a working university portal as permission to miss the initial submission.

  1. 1Read the admission requirements for the shortlisted PhD programmes and assess research fit.
  2. 2Prepare the initial RGC application with the intended programme choices.
  3. 3Submit that initial application before noon HKT and save the reference number.
  4. 4Enter the number in each separate university PhD application and submit its documents.
  5. 5Check the university's referee status and retain both types of confirmation.

Official reference: RGC initial and university application instructions

Choose programmes by research fit, not a ranking alone

For each choice, identify the question you want to study, the relevant academic group and the programme's instructions on contacting supervisors. Do not assume that rules at one university apply to another. Prepare a short account of what you have already done and what support or training the proposed work requires.

A useful comparison has a reason for each choice. If both programmes require the same missing laboratory background, two applications may not provide a practical fallback. A second choice can still demand an independently convincing academic match and a complete set of references.

Frequently asked questions

Is the HKPFS initial form the full university application?+

No. Obtain the HKPFS reference number first, then submit full applications to your selected universities, quoting that number and meeting their individual requirements.

How many universities can I choose?+

RGC permits up to two programmes or departments at one or two universities. Read the choice and amendment instructions before final submission.

Does HKPFS automatically waive every university fee?+

Check the selected university's package. RGC's scheme support and university tuition waivers, top-ups or extra years are separate provisions. A general funding label does not establish an application-fee waiver.

What is the HKPFS initial deadline in Pakistan?+

1 December 2026 at 12:00 noon HKT is 1 December at 09:00 Pakistan Standard Time. The later university deadline does not extend that initial deadline.
