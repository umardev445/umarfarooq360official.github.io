GIST graduate scholarship: funding components, fees and admission tasks

GIST’s international graduate route handles scholarship support with admission, but that does not make every charge disappear or every allowance unconditional. Check the current intake and read each funding component separately.

Updated 23 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

The GIST FAQ says international graduate scholarships do not need a separate application. It also requires application-fee payment for a valid application and lists a one-timeKRW 680,000 matriculation fee. Current funding amounts and exceptions need the applicable guide and award terms.

Next step: Use the official graduate schedule and document list for your intended intake, then record the application payment and the conditions on each funding component.

The scholarship process does not replace a valid admission application

The international graduate FAQ says scholarship consideration requires no separate scholarship application. It also says an online application is not processed as valid until its designated application fee has been paid. Keep those two facts together: automatic scholarship handling is not permission to skip admission requirements or payment.

Use the owner’s current schedule to identify the intended intake and permitted submission period. This review does not turn the presence of an online admissions website into confirmation that a particular 2027 round is open. Record the exact call and category before arranging documents. A general programme page and a currently accepting intake are different pieces of evidence.

Do not combine a tuition waiver with a promise of zero cost

The owner FAQ identifies a one-timeKRW 680,000 matriculation fee. The support page notes possible exemptions, which should be checked for the individual case rather than assumed. Admission-related charges are distinct from tuition support, and the application fee is a separate payment again.

Create a ledger before accepting or paying: charge, due date, exemption or support basis, and amount you must supply. If an exemption is pending, keep the full charge in the confirmed-cost scenario. A positive scholarship description cannot settle when a particular fee is due or whether the bank transfer cost is included.

Read the conditions and age of the funding table

GIST’s public support page separates tuition, student and meal allowances, international allowance, project research assistance, insurance and flight reimbursement. It attaches credit or GPA conditions to some allowances. It also explicitly labels assistantship averages and housing figures as 2022 data. Those dated numbers should not be presented as guaranteed 2027 rates.

Use the table as a map of questions, then request the current applicable figures and terms where necessary. Keep a project-dependent research payment separate from an unconditional monthly award. Check reimbursement timing before treating travel support as money available to buy the ticket. Housing availability, utilities and deposits also need their own current evidence.

Make the document set consistent across the degree stages

The graduate document list and FAQ should be read together for the intended application. Record the academic record, degree or expected-graduation evidence, translations or certification and any later originals required. A final-semester applicant needs to distinguish the evidence accepted for applying from final documents needed after admission.

An original document register can use four fields: issuing institution, degree stage, document status and required destination. A doctoral applicant with two degrees should not assume that one master’s transcript resolves every bachelor’s record task. Keep names and dates consistent, and ask the admissions office about a genuine discrepancy before altering a document. Use the exact current document list instead of a generic scholarship checklist.

Original funding test: what cash is needed before the first payment?

Consider a fictional applicant whose tuition is supported but who must make an application payment, settle a matriculation charge and buy travel before any reimbursement. A budget that starts only with monthly rent misses the first decision the applicant must make.

List every pre-arrival payment with its actual due date. Add only confirmed exemptions or benefits available by that date. In a separate line, show later reimbursement without spending it twice. Do not insert an invented exchange rate: keep the Korean-won commitments inKRW and any ticket quote in its quoted currency until you use a dated conversion for personal planning.

Check the official status and preserve a useful question

The FAQ directs applicants to the online system for application status and does not offer email or post as a replacement submission method. If a technical problem occurs, record the error and use the published admissions contact; an email reporting the problem is not itself a valid application.

A focused funding question names the intended intake, degree and unclear component: for example, whether a dated public allowance table remains applicable or when a reimbursement can be claimed. Do not send bank credentials or a complete personal file in an initial enquiry. Retain the official reply with the current guide and eventual award conditions so the final budget reflects evidence rather than a copied headline.

Frequently asked questions

Does GIST require a separate scholarship application?+

The international graduate FAQ says scholarships are granted without a separate application process. Complete the correct admission application and all its requirements.

Does scholarship handling remove the application fee?+

No. The FAQ says the application is not valid for processing until the designated application fee is paid.

Can I use the public housing or assistantship figures as 2027 rates?+

Some figures are explicitly labelled 2022. Check current applicable amounts and project or academic conditions instead of presenting those dated values as guaranteed 2027 support.
