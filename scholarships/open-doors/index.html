Choose the track that fits your next qualification

The 2026 rules link eligibility to age and qualification, assessed against the registration-opening conditions. Check both columns: having the required degree does not remove the age condition. Applicants must meet the full rule for their track.

For the degree tracks, the rules address both completed qualifications and those due in the competition academic year. Postdoctoral applicants must already hold the relevant research degree at registration opening. Use the official wording for your exact situation.

Separate the base scholarship from conditional extra support

The main official FAQ describes tuition coverage and a university-dependent stipend, while personal costs remain the student’s responsibility. The organiser’s August announcement also describes possible additional government grant support for participants enrolling in Russian-language programmes. These are not identical guarantees.

Ask the chosen university which support applies to your track, language and award. Until you have confirmation, budget for housing, food, insurance, travel and arrival costs yourself. Do not assume that the phrase “fully funded” means all of these are automatically free.

Build a relevant portfolio, not a certificate collection

Start with the subject area and track criteria. For each achievement, ask what it demonstrates: subject knowledge, research skills, an original output or preparation for the next degree. A smaller evidence file that can be understood is better than many unrelated attachments.

Use accurate titles, dates and descriptions. If a project was a team effort, state your contribution rather than taking credit for the whole result. Check translation and file requirements in the account. Motivation should connect your evidence with the selected subject, not simply repeat a desire to study abroad.

A practical motivation-letter plan

Use the official prompt in your account as the authority. As a drafting method, organise notes around preparation, academic purpose, evidence and the next step. For doctoral applications, research direction and supervision fit deserve more attention than a long biography.

Teaching example

From achievement to subject fit

Evidence: I analysed a small dataset for my final project and documented the cleaning process. Learning gap: I need deeper training in experimental design and reproducible analysis. Subject fit: I will compare the subject-area curriculum and university research groups against those gaps before deciding my preferences.

Original fictional planning notes, not an official winning letter. Replace them with your own evidence and the actual prompt.

Prepare for the second stage before the result

The official procedure uses proctored subject tasks for the degree tracks. Preparation should use the organiser’s syllabus and samples, not unofficial “leaked” questions. Test your computer, connection, camera and identity-document access before the scheduled session.

Record the account’s separate second-stage registration and time-slot instructions. Advancing from stage one is not the same as being registered for stage two. If different subject sessions clash, resolve the choice before the last day. Follow the rules on permitted resources and independent work.

University preferences and winning are separate steps

Check which participating institution offers your intended programme, teaching language and research environment. A university’s reputation does not establish that it offers the exact course you want. Keep a preference sheet with the programme URL and an unresolved-questions column.

After a qualifying result, follow the organiser’s document-transfer and admission instructions. A competition result does not remove later documentation, enrolment or immigration responsibilities. Research-track applicants should distinguish a supervisor’s interview from a final university admission decision.

Plan for an offer, an unsuccessful result or a technical problem

Keep a budget and a second funding option while waiting. If you encounter a submission problem, contact the project office with a concise description and permitted supporting screenshot; do not send the same request through many channels.

Existing candidates should record the next action in their official account. New applicants can prepare using the portfolio and budgeting guidance but must wait for a future call and check its rules separately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I register for Open Doors 2026 now?+

The published 1 November 2026 registration deadline has passed. Later results and stage-booking dates concern existing candidates. Check the organiser for any official amendment or future call.

Does Open Doors cover every living cost?+

Do not assume that. The main FAQ describes tuition and a university-dependent stipend; additional grant support has separate conditions. Confirm the package that applies to your actual offer.

Is 13 November another application deadline?+

The current schedule identifies 13 November 2026 as the first-stage result date. Registration is listed as closing on 1 November.

Do all tracks have the same stages?+

No. Doctoral and postdoctoral routes involve additional interviews and different research-related preparation.
