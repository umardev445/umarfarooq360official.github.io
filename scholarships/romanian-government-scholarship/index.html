Romanian Government Scholarship: choose the language and document route

Start with the degree and language you would actually study. A scholarship headline cannot answer whether this route matches your programme, qualification and preparation time.

Updated 23 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

The MFA page still shows the closed 2026 call. Bachelor’s and master’s awards use Romanian; doctoral study can use a language set by the school. Read exclusions and the next call before applying.

The reviewed call is historical, so choose a preparation task

The owner displays 16 February–31 March 2026 for its application period. Those dates are past on 23 November 2026. A visible account or application button does not establish a new call. This guide is for preparing a reasoned choice and a checkable file; it does not announce 2027 applications.

First decide what remains useful before a new announcement: reading programme options, obtaining accurate academic records and identifying a real training need. Keep any old checklist labelled with its cycle. When new instructions appear, compare the requirements before paying for time-sensitive documents or assuming the previous deadline has shifted by one year.

Test whether the degree-language route fits your plan

The MFA scheme is for non-EU applicants subject to stated exclusions; Medicine, Dental Medicine and Pharmacy are excluded. It provides Romanian-taught bachelor’s and master’s routes, with language preparation where applicable. A doctoral school can specify a different study language and requires tutor agreement under the published scheme.

Use the language question as an academic decision. In a fictional case, an applicant wants an English-taught master’s because they cannot commit to Romanian study. They should research another suitable route instead of assuming an English-language application portal makes the funded degree English-taught. Another applicant willing to study in Romanian should plan the preparation period alongside the intended degree rather than treating it as a minor administrative formality.

Make the academic case before making a document bundle

Write a short degree-choice note containing the subject you want to learn, a preparation gap from your previous study and a realistic use for the training. This is an editorial exercise, not an official application essay. It helps you avoid choosing a programme solely because a scholarship website lists it.

For a fictional engineering applicant, the useful gap might be learning how to evaluate measurement uncertainty after a small project produced inconsistent readings. The next research task is to identify actual programmes addressing that gap. Do not invent modules, laboratory access or employment outcomes to strengthen the note. Mark each programme feature with the owner page where you found it.

Use a dependency sheet for translations and academic records

Before requesting a service, record each document’s issuing authority, original language, exact requirement and next dependent action. The published scheme requires academic records and identity evidence, with specified authorised translations and authentication rules. Read the full owner list for your situation rather than treating this worksheet as its replacement.

An original transcript that still contains a name error should be corrected through the issuer before translation. Translating an incorrect record can create two versions that require correction. If two receiving tasks request different formats, keep their requirements separate and obtain clarification before ordering one expensive package on an informal assurance.

Keep a private review copy of the exact file you submit

Create a local cycle-labelled folder with an untouched original, a working copy and the final submitted version. Compare spelling, dates and qualification titles across your CV and supporting documents. Use clear filenames that let you identify the content without opening every file.

Before submission in an actual open call, reopen each generated file. Check that all pages are legible and that a signature or reverse-side explanation has not been lost. Save the official receipt and record any later request separately. A file uploaded to a draft is not automatically evidence that the application was submitted.

Plan after selection without treating the award as arrival completed

If selected, read the actual acceptance and university instructions before making travel commitments. Keep award acceptance, final enrolment, accommodation and immigration steps as separate tasks with their own owners. Ask the responsible institution to clarify a condition instead of inferring it from an unofficial group message.

Prepare two cash scenarios: one with confirmed support available on its stated date and another for costs that arise earlier. Use your own quotes for travel and temporary accommodation. This method does not claim a benefit amount or guarantee housing. Its purpose is to reveal a timing gap while you can still plan for it.
