KAUST Graduate Admission and Fellowship 2027 guide

KAUST offers research-intensive science and engineering graduate study with admitted students supported through the KAUST Fellowship. The Spring 2027 PhD round is closed; Fall 2027 applications for MS, MS/PhD and PhD study are open until 3 January 2027.

Updated 3 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Fall 2027 MS, MS/PhD and PhD applications close on 3 January 2027; the Spring PhD deadline has passed. Fellowship support normally follows admission, with sponsorship exceptions. Check the current English rules, which do not accept a general MOI certificate.

Entry requirements and current English rules

The current admissions page requires the relevant academic qualification and a minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0 or equivalent. Its standard IELTS requirement is 7.0 overall with at least 6.5 in each section. It also lists other accepted tests and conditional provisions for specified applicants; check the current table for your case.

A medium-of-instruction certificate is not accepted as English evidence. The page defines exemptions for qualifying in-person degrees in named countries and at KAUST. Do not assume that a Pakistani English-taught degree alone grants that exemption. A minimum score establishes a requirement, not a predicted admission chance.

KAUST Fellowship: admission route, not a second award form

The current FAQ says there is no separate KAUST Fellowship application. Admitted students normally receive it, with exceptions for external sponsorship or partnership arrangements. Declare existing substantial support and use the offer to establish the package that applies to you.

The funding page lists full tuition and bench-fee coverage, accommodation, health insurance, relocation assistance and a stipend. It states annual stipend values of US$20,000 for MS and US$25,000–30,000 for PhD, depending on qualifications and progress. These are annual values, not monthly salary amounts. Housing upgrades and personal circumstances can create additional costs.

Prepare the research application and referee requests

The application asks for academic records, a CV, a statement of purpose, identification and three referee contacts. Use academic or professional institutional email addresses as required. Current instructions say reference invitations are sent when details are entered, so ask permission before triggering the email.

Build the statement around work you can explain: the question, your role, a method, a result and a limitation. Connect that preparation to the intended graduate programme. A claim that KAUST has excellent facilities becomes more useful when you explain which training or research direction you need and why.

Submit the graduate route in the correct order

The graduate application and the Visiting Student Research Program serve different purposes. Do not use an internship form to seek master's or PhD admission. Check the graduate programme and intake shown on every screen.

  1. 1Choose MS, MS/PhD or PhD and read the current programme requirements.
  2. 2Resolve English evidence and collect the academic record and research materials.
  3. 3Confirm referee availability before entering their contacts.
  4. 4Complete the graduate application before 3 January and monitor the references.
  5. 5Retain confirmation and follow interview, offer and enrolment instructions.

A practical plan for applicants from Pakistan

Start by checking whether your qualification meets the chosen degree route, then read the current test and exemption rules. Obtain the grading scale and any required translation rather than using a self-converted GPA as official evidence. If final results are pending, follow the published provisional-record instructions and the eventual final-document requirement.

Keep a small upfront-cost plan for testing, documents and other expenses incurred before enrolment. The fellowship FAQ says language-test costs are not covered. Separate that expense from relocation support in the eventual award. Ask admissions about unclear degree or sponsorship details using your programme name and exact qualification.

Frequently asked questions

Is a separate KAUST Fellowship form required?+

The current FAQ says no separate application is needed. Admitted students normally receive fellowship support, except in specified sponsorship or partnership arrangements. Your offer establishes the actual package.

Is a Pakistani medium-of-instruction certificate sufficient?+

KAUST's current entry page says it does not accept MOI certificates. Use an accepted test or a specifically defined exemption, and read any conditional provisions that apply to your applicant category.

Are the published stipend amounts monthly?+

No. The funding page describes US$20,000 per year for MS and US$25,000–30,000 per year for PhD, depending on qualifications and progress. Distinguish an annual value from the monthly payment.

Is this the same application as KAUST VSRP?+

No. This guide concerns graduate degree admission and fellowship support. VSRP is a separate visiting research internship with its own process.
