Scholarships in Germany for international students

German funding is spread across DAAD programmes, universities, foundations and paid research routes. Start with the applicant category and the organisation that actually owns the application.

Updated 22 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

The short answer

There is no single Germany scholarship application. Search the DAAD database for applicant-specific programmes, check university awards separately and treat paid doctoral positions as a different funding route.

Next step: Choose one route below, open its current official notice and record the application owner, coverage and deadline before preparing documents.

Use the DAAD database as a filter, not a promise

Select the country from which you are applying, your current academic status, subject and funding purpose. Open each programme record and verify whether the scholarship owner or the host course receives the application.

A programme appearing in a database does not establish personal eligibility. Save the current programme notice and check degree, graduation date, work experience, language, nomination and deadline requirements together.

Bachelor's funding needs a wider plan

Major German scholarship databases contain far more postgraduate and research opportunities than full-course undergraduate awards. Bachelor's applicants should also inspect the chosen university's international office, admission scholarship notices and any institution-administered Deutschlandstipendium call.

Do not treat low tuition as full funding. Semester contributions, housing, insurance, food, travel and visa proof of funds can remain payable even when tuition is limited.

Separate scholarships from paid doctoral positions

A doctoral route can be financed through a scholarship, a structured programme or an employment contract attached to a research project. Compare monthly support or salary, social-security treatment, teaching duties, funding duration and whether admission to a doctoral programme is separate.

Use the official Research in Germany routes and the host institution's vacancy page. Contact a supervisor only when you can describe a specific research fit.

Build a funding comparison before accepting

Record confirmed tuition support, monthly cash, insurance, travel, family support and the payment start date. Then list costs that remain yours. A headline award amount is not enough to show whether arrival cash or the monthly budget works.

Only subtract funding that is confirmed in writing. Keep a separate contingency for housing deposits and expenses due before the first scholarship payment.

Frequently asked questions

Does Germany have one scholarship application for international students?+

No. DAAD programmes, universities, foundations and research employers use different eligibility rules, deadlines and submission routes.

Is studying at a public German university automatically fully funded?+

No. Low or no tuition does not pay living expenses, semester contributions, insurance, travel or visa-related proof of funds.

Where should I search for a DAAD scholarship?+

Use the official DAAD scholarship database, filter it for your applicant profile and then read the current programme record and application instructions.

Are PhD positions the same as scholarships?+

Not always. Some doctoral researchers hold employment contracts, while others receive scholarships or programme funding. Compare the legal and financial terms of the exact offer.
