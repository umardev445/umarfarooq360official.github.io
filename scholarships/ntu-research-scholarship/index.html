NTU Research Scholarship: choose a research programme and check the award

Start with an actual research degree and school. The scholarship’s central terms, a school’s application window and your personal cost plan describe different decisions; use all three before treating a place as funded.

Updated 19 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Choose a full-time research programme and verify its school-specific RSS availability and deadline. NTU’s current terms distinguish tuition, stipend, renewal and duties; indicating scholarship interest is not an award. The NBS August 2027 window is one specific example, not every school’s deadline.

Next step: Save the chosen programme’s current instructions and complete the programme-identity and research-fit worksheets before relying on a central deadline.

Identify the school and research degree before using a deadline

The NTU Research Scholarship supports graduate research rather than every taught master’s programme carrying NTU’s name. The central RSS page describes full-time research candidates and a minimum Honours(Distinction) or equivalent; admission and scholarship assessment still depend on the chosen programme and school. A central eligibility statement does not establish that a particular school has funding for every research master’s course.

Choose one degree record and save its school, intake, research area and application route. Then check the school’s own instructions for evidence and available support. If you are comparing a coursework master’s with a PhD, keep separate records: the programme structures, selection criteria and funding arrangements should not be merged into a single “NTU scholarship” application.

Read an August 2027 example without applying it to every school

Nanyang Business School explicitly lists its RSS application period for August 2027 as 1 October 2026–31 January 2027. That is a useful named example, not a university-wide deadline. The central research guide also lists earlier school-specific cutoffs, including 15 November for several arts, humanities, social-science and communication schools. At the 19 November review that listed November date had passed; a January date elsewhere does not reopen it.

Record the exact programme deadline and whether the supporting documents must arrive by the same point. If the school’s instructions and a central annual table appear inconsistent, ask the named school using your intended intake. Do not choose the more convenient date. Where a source gives only a calendar date, obtain its closing-time instructions before relying on an exact-time conversion.

Use an evidence map for research fit

Create a short research-fit map before writing your statement. In a fictional engineering case, an applicant has used a simulation method in a final-year project and wants to investigate its limits on a different dataset. The useful evidence is the question, the method they actually used, one result and the limitation they can explain. “I am passionate about innovation” does not supply that evidence.

Add why the proposed school or group is suitable, using its actual research rather than copying a profile. Mark methods you have not learned as development needs. Follow the programme’s instructions about supervisor contact, proposal, personal statement, references and any video or test requirement. A positive faculty conversation can help you refine a plan, but it is not an admission offer or scholarship decision.

Read the stipend and support period as written

The central RSS table effectiveJanuary 2026 lists international-student monthly stipends of S$3,000 for PhD and S$2,900 for research master’s candidates, alongside annual tuition coverage. These are the current published rates, not a newly announced 2027 increase. The award is initially tenable for one year and renewal depends on progress; the stated maximum support is two years for a master’s and four for a PhD, subject to the school and research funding.

The admission guide’s maximum candidature and the scholarship’s maximum support need not be the same. Put both in your record and ask how any additional period would be financed. Do not budget the maximum stipend, a possible post-qualifying increase or an extension as already awarded. Your offer and applicable award conditions determine actual coverage and continuation.

No bond does not mean no scholarship duties

NTU states that RSS has no bond, while international scholars must fulfil Graduate Assistantship Programme teaching or laboratory-supervision duties. Those statements concern different obligations and can both be true. Before accepting, read the applicable duties, academic expectations and approval rules rather than interpreting “no bond” as freedom from all scholarship conditions.

Make an original weekly planning record with research, required coursework and scholarship duties on separate lines. Use it to identify questions about workload and supervision; do not invent an official hours allocation. If an obligation is unclear, ask the school how it is scheduled and documented. Keep written clarification with the award terms so a later assumption does not become a dispute.

Prepare cash before the first payment

A monthly stipend does not settle the timing of every arrival expense. NTU asks new students to update Singapore bank details before the 15th of the commencement month to avoid stipend delays. That administrative instruction is not a promise that a student without completed arrangements will receive money on a chosen day.

Use a fictional arrival worksheet: assume S$1,200 refundable housing deposit,S$800 travel and S$1,000 initial living costs. The initial cash requirement is S$3,000 before any confirmed reimbursement or payment. Keep the deposit’s return conditions separate from spending and replace all assumptions with real quotes. List the account, registration and award tasks on which payment depends, then decide how to cover the interval if any task takes longer.

Check the admission form and scholarship interest together

NTU’s scholarship FAQ says a separate scholarship application is not required for the described process: applicants indicate interest in the admission application and the school assesses them. Follow the actual form and chosen programme; ticking interest is a request for consideration, not an award. Retain the complete submitted application and any later instructions.

Before submitting, inspect the exact degree/intake, evidence fields, reference arrangements and scholarship selection. Keep admission and funding outcomes as separate statuses until the offer explains both. If you receive an offer, read duration, tuition, stipend, duties and continuation conditions together, then compare them with your budget. Use the owner’s current instructions for any acceptance or banking step; this guide has not tested an authenticated application account.

Frequently asked questions

Is RSS for any NTU taught master’s?+

No. Its central scope concerns graduate research. Check the exact school and degree for funding availability rather than transferring RSS terms to a coursework course.

Does no bond mean no duties?+

No. The central page also requires international scholars to fulfil Graduate Assistantship Programme duties. Read the actual award conditions.

Can I use 31 January for every school?+

No. The central guide lists school-specific exceptions, and the NBS date here belongs to its namedAugust 2027 route.
