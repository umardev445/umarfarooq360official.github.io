ETH Zurich ESOP Scholarship 2027: eligibility and pre-proposal

ETH Zurich's Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme supports excellent master's applicants with funding, mentorship and a tuition waiver. For autumn 2027, prepare the ESOP materials within the first master's application window and develop your own thesis pre-proposal.

Updated 3 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

ETH publishes the ESOP window as 1–30 November 2026 for autumn 2027. From that intake, support is CHF13,500 per semester plus a tuition waiver. A top-10% bachelor's result and an independently developed thesis pre-proposal are central requirements.

Next step: Confirm your master's eligibility, read the pre-proposal PDF and check the exact portal closing time. The scholarship page prints the time as '11.59h, MEZ'; do not assume a midnight cutoff.

Who is eligible, and which application counts?

Apply through the master's eApply route during its first application window. Applicants already enrolled in an ETH master's or holding a master's degree are not eligible for ESOP. Admission to an eligible degree and award of the scholarship remain separate decisions.

Check the degree programme's language, academic prerequisites and application documents before preparing the scholarship extras. Do not interpret top 10% as a promise of selection or convert your GPA with an arbitrary online formula. Use the grading scale and any reliable cohort context your university can provide.

Official reference: ETH ESOP eligibility and first-window rule

Write your own ESOP thesis pre-proposal

ETH's linked guidance sets a maximum of 500 words excluding references for the pre-proposal. It asks for a possible research project with a question, context, method and realistic milestones. The final thesis topic is developed later during the degree; the application is an opportunity to show how you think about research.

Choose a topic you can defend. Read and cite the work you rely on, distinguish an existing result from your proposed contribution and describe what would make the project feasible. ETH explicitly requires independent authorship and established citation practice. Use feedback to improve clarity without outsourcing the intellectual work.

Reference invitations and submission sequence

If the degree programme does not already request references, ESOP requires two professor referees and describes system invitations with a two-week response period. Follow the particular portal instructions and track receipt; do not assume an emailed promise is a submitted reference.

  1. 1Check the master's prerequisites and whether ESOP eligibility applies to you.
  2. 2Develop the pre-proposal from sources you have read and an achievable question.
  3. 3Confirm suitable referees and prepare the programme's admission documents.
  4. 4Complete the ESOP part of eApply in the first window and inspect reference instructions.
  5. 5Submit early, save confirmation and monitor any interview or reference requests.

Official reference: ETH reference instructions

A realistic research and funding plan

Consider a fictional applicant interested in detecting errors in a small environmental dataset. A proposal to solve all climate prediction would overstate the available evidence. A narrower comparison of one baseline and one justified method, with a clear evaluation rule, is easier to assess and discuss.

The same discipline applies to the budget. Keep the semester grant, tuition waiver, housing deposit and spending before the first payment in separate rows. Verify any dependants' costs or personal travel individually. A total scholarship headline should not become a promise that every optional expense is reimbursed.

Worked example

Before finalising the pre-proposal, explain it aloud in two minutes: the question, the evidence, your method, the main limitation and the expected output. If you cannot explain how the method answers the question, revise the plan before polishing its wording.

Final ESOP checklist

The live eApply instructions are the authority for submission mechanics and closing time. Record the exact time you see instead of interpreting an ambiguous clock notation.

Frequently asked questions

How much does ETH ESOP pay for 2027 entry?+

ETH specifies CHF13,500 per semester from autumn 2027, plus a tuition fee waiver, for the regular three- or four-semester master's duration. Check your offer for the actual payment arrangements.

Can I apply if I already hold a master's degree?+

The ESOP page excludes applicants who hold a master's degree and those already enrolled in an ETH master's. Check the current eligibility rule before investing in the application.

Is the ESOP pre-proposal my final thesis topic?+

No. ETH describes it as an independently developed possible project that demonstrates research planning. The final topic is defined during the master's with the supervisor.

Can I use a later master's application window for ESOP?+

ETH requires ESOP applications in the first application window. A later admission opportunity does not reopen the scholarship window.
