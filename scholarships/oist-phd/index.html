OIST PhD Scholarship 2027: funding and application guide

OIST's funded PhD route is a degree application to the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology Graduate University in Japan. Apply to the PhD programme, identify relevant faculty and prepare for the admissions workshop; do not confuse it with OIST's separate research internship.

Updated 3 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

The current PhD round closes on 15 November 2026 at 23:59 JST (UTC+9). It leads to the 2027 online admissions workshop. The application fee is ¥5,000, and at least two references must reach the system before you can submit.

Next step: Read the PhD instructions, ask eligible referees and check that your degree can be completed before the intended enrolment date.

Eligibility and English-language evidence

A bachelor's, master's or equivalent qualification must be completed before admission. OIST admits to the programme rather than promising a place in a named professor's unit. Read the available research areas and explain why the programme's approach fits the work you want to learn.

The official FAQ permits evidence of English-medium education for the language-test exemption. Other applicants should follow the PhD page's TOEFL or IELTS instructions. Do not infer a waiver from nationality, or import the research internship's English-test rules into this degree application.

Official reference: OIST PhD eligibility · OIST FAQ: English-medium evidence and faculty selection

Funding does not mean every upfront expense disappears

OIST describes its five-year PhD as fully funded and its FAQ explains support through a research assistantship. The same FAQ says students cannot take outside part-time work. Use the offer and current terms to evaluate your own living arrangements, rather than planning to close an unknown budget gap with another job.

List the application charge, document preparation and any expenses incurred before support begins. For each cost, distinguish an included benefit, a later reimbursement and a payment you must make yourself. The current PhD application page states that personal financial circumstances do not qualify for a fee waiver; use OIST's contact instructions if sanctions prevent payment.

Official reference: OIST admissions overview · OIST support and outside-work FAQ

Prepare a research-fit statement you can discuss

Choose a research question you understand well enough to explain without slides. Connect a past project to a question you now want to investigate, then identify the methods or training you lack. A list of famous laboratories does less work than a clear connection between the question and the programme.

The published statement instructions list both 800 words and 3,000 English characters. Check the live form and satisfy both limits; do not assume that a document under the word count will fit into a character-limited field. Keep optional publications and test results separate from required evidence.

Teaching example

A research-fit planning prompt

In [your actual project], I investigated [question] using [method]. The result left [specific limitation]. I now want to learn [approach] to examine [next question], and have identified [relevant OIST research area] as a possible training fit.

Original planning scaffold. Replace the brackets with your own work; this is not an accepted statement or a promise of admission.

Reference and submission workflow

Use referees who can discuss the research ability they have observed. Follow the current rules on eligible recommenders and direct system submission. Being able to upload your CV does not mean the reference dependency has been satisfied.

  1. 1Check degree timing and identify relevant faculty from the programme's published research information.
  2. 2Create the PhD application and send eligible referees their system invitations.
  3. 3Prepare the academic record, statement, CV, language evidence and identification.
  4. 4Check receipt of at least two references, the live form limits and payment instructions.
  5. 5Review the complete application, submit and retain the confirmation.

Worked example

A fictional final-year applicant expects to graduate in June 2027. They should not select May enrolment on that assumption. They can investigate the September or January options and provide accurate expected-completion evidence, subject to OIST accepting their qualification and timing.

Frequently asked questions

Is the OIST PhD application free?+

No. The current official PhD page lists a non-refundable ¥5,000 application fee. This is separate from the funding provided after admission.

Can a bachelor's graduate apply directly to OIST's PhD?+

Yes, the published route accepts a completed bachelor's, master's or equivalent qualification before admission. Academic fit and competitive selection still apply.

Do I need a professor's acceptance before applying?+

OIST asks applicants to identify faculty of interest but admits to the programme. Its FAQ advises against contacting faculty to obtain approval or support before applying; follow the central admissions process.

What is the OIST deadline in Pakistan?+

15 November 2026 at 23:59 JST converts to 15 November at 19:59 Pakistan Standard Time. Complete the submission and reference requirements before that time.
