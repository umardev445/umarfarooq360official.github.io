UBC International Scholars Program 2027: application guide

The UBC International Scholars Program combines academic achievement, community leadership and demonstrated financial need. For September 2027 entry, complete UBC admission first, then coordinate the award form with your school nominator and a separate teacher referee.

Updated 10 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

The 2027 award application closes on 2 December 2026 at 12:00 Pacific Time (deadline extended). UBC assesses financial need; a fixed full scholarship is not guaranteed. Admission submission and both recommendation forms are dependencies of the award application.

Next step: Confirm that your school can nominate you, choose two different recommenders and complete your UBC admission application.

Eligibility: school background, need and course choice

UBC requires international study-permit status, direct entry from an accredited secondary school and strong academic results. For this intake, high-school graduation must be June 2026 or later. The official page lists excluded degree combinations and requires the relevant English-language admission standard.

Read your intended degree name carefully against those exclusions. A general statement that international students can apply does not establish eligibility for a nursing, joint or other excluded programme. If your educational pathway is unusual, obtain an answer from UBC before preparing financial records.

Nomination and admission: complete the steps in order

Your application depends on other people's submissions. Ask about the school's campus-specific nomination allocation before treating a willingness to write a letter as an actual nomination. Send each person the correct invitation and agree a follow-up date.

  1. 1Choose the campus and first-choice programme, and confirm the school's nomination process.
  2. 2Complete the UBC admission application and retain its confirmation.
  3. 3Create the award application and invite the nominator and separate teacher referee.
  4. 4Prepare the financial evidence and your own answers; track both recommendation forms.
  5. 5Submit the completed award application and save the final confirmation.

Worked example

Suggested planning exercise: if you choose 12 November as your own submission target, ask the school when its internal selection and recommendation tasks must finish. That target is your buffer, not a new UBC deadline. A reminder from you cannot substitute for a referee's submitted form.

Prepare financial evidence without guessing an award amount

Build a private evidence folder for household income, expenses, assets, dependants and available support. Keep the underlying documents and record the period and currency used. An unexplained mismatch between an essay and a financial form can be harder to resolve than a modest but accurately documented budget.

Do not treat the estimated award as money already available. Calculate the cost of attendance and the resources your family can actually contribute, then explain the difference consistently. Ask UBC how to document irregular income or unavailable records rather than inventing a salary or a conversion rate.

Make leadership evidence specific

Choose an example where you can describe the problem, your own contribution and what changed. A title such as club president gives little information without the work behind it. Distinguish the team's achievement from the part you personally completed.

For a fictional tutoring project, useful evidence might be the attendance record you maintained, the teaching material you created and a change you made after feedback. Do not invent a success percentage. Explain what you learned and why the proposed degree helps you address the next problem. Write your own answers to the current form rather than submitting a generic scholarship essay.

After the award application: admission documents and English evidence

UBC gives International Scholars applicants a 31 January document and English-standard deadline. Read its instructions for records that are not yet available. That later document stage does not extend the extended December award application deadline.

Keep a separate task for official delivery: document name, sender, request date and receipt status. Monitor the admissions email as well as the award platform. If invited to a video interview, follow the invitation's response window; do not treat an invitation as an award offer.

Check the submission, then save the evidence

A saved draft and a submitted application are different states. Reopen the application summary, inspect every attachment and check the referee indicators. Keep the confirmation email or reference number with the version of the instructions you used. Put any later transcript, offer-acceptance or enrolment task in a separate calendar entry.

The tracker is an editable planning sheet, not an application form or eligibility assessment. Its suggested next actions belong to you; deadlines and requirements remain controlled by the institution. Replace the blank owner, internal deadline and confirmation fields with your own plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is UBC International Scholars automatically fully funded?+

No fixed package applies to every applicant. UBC assesses financial need and the applicable award conditions. Use the official assessment and eventual award letter when planning what remains to pay.

Can the same person nominate me and provide the teacher reference?+

No. UBC requires different people for these roles. Confirm the school nomination before inviting them through the application system.

Can I finish the scholarship form before applying for admission?+

UBC's current sequence requires admission submission first. You also cannot finish the award application until the nominator and teacher referee have submitted their forms.

What time is the deadline in Pakistan?+

The extended 2 December 2026 noon Pacific deadline converts to 3 December at 01:00 Pakistan Standard Time. UBC labels the deadline as extended; verify its current portal before submitting. Submit early and use the official portal's clock.
