NUS Research Scholarship: admission, stipend and school-specific tasks

The NUS Research Scholarship is a research-degree route. The funding request belongs with the graduate admission application, while the intended faculty or school controls its own application deadline and additional requirements.

Updated 23 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

NUS lists PhD and research master’s students as the qualification types. Applicants indicate scholarship interest within GradApp. Tuition is waived under the award, stipend rates depend on category, and international scholars have Graduate Assistantship Programme requirements.

Next step: Open the intended research programme’s faculty instructions, then record its admission deadline, evidence requirements and scholarship-interest step.

Confirm a research degree before comparing funding

The scholarship page identifies PhD and master’s by research applicants. A broad line about graduate programmes should not be used to extend this award to every taught or coursework degree. Start with the exact programme type, school and intended intake, then check how that unit handles admission and scholarship consideration.

School-level availability can be narrower than the central award description. The Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health currently says its research MSc applicants must be self-financing because no scholarships are available for them, while it lists competitive PhD awards. A student must therefore check the actual degree and school before building a funded budget. Similar degree names do not prove the same funding route.

Read the category and effective date on the stipend table

The owner’s rates effective 1 January 2026 list international stipends ofSGD 3,000 monthly forPhD andSGD 2,900 forresearch master’s students, alongside a full tuition waiver. Other citizenship or residence categories have different rates. Check the current owner table and eventual offer rather than treating a figure for another category as your entitlement.

The page describes an initial one-year tenure, later renewal subject to progress and university discretion, with stated maximum periods. Those limits are not an unconditional promise of cash for the entire degree. Build a budget from the actual award period, and keep personal or family costs separate when they are not expressly included.

Separate a school requirement from a general scholarship overview

NUS’s central page sends applicants to faculty websites for the January and August intake deadlines. It does not supply one deadline that this guide can safely assign to every department. The same caution applies to test or language requirements: a general overview should not be used to erase a mandatory condition in the intended school’s application instructions.

Use a two-column requirements record. In the first column, copy the current programme requirement in your own concise notes and retain its source. In the second, identify the document or action that will prove completion. A test you have booked is not a score received; a referee invited is not a reference received. Mark those differences before the final submission review.

Original worksheet: show fit without claiming a result in advance

Imagine a candidate interested in modelling urban heat. Their first draft promises to prove that one design solves the problem. A stronger research starting point asks which observations would distinguish the proposed explanation from alternatives, what data can be accessed and which methods the group can support.

The worksheet below separates preparation from aspiration. Use a real project to show what you already know, state the method you need to develop and identify a concrete connection with the intended research environment. Do not invent a supervisor’s commitment or imply that reading a paper gives you access to its dataset. The purpose is a credible research conversation, not a pre-written conclusion.

Submit the funding interest in the admission application

NUS directs incoming students to indicate scholarship interest within their graduate application on GradApp and complete additional scholarship requirements there. A saved scholarship page or an email to a potential supervisor is not a submitted funding request. Use the authenticated system reached from the owner’s site.

Before submitting, inspect the full application record with the correct school, intake and research direction. Check consistency across the CV, statement, transcripts and references. Save the final confirmation and note any items that must arrive independently. If an expected attachment is missing, follow the school’s current procedure; do not assume that submitting a form automatically grants an extension for every outstanding item.

Budget and obligations after an offer

The published terms require international scholars to fulfil Graduate Assistantship Programme duties and require prior university approval for other work or awards. Read the applicable agreement before accepting an outside commitment. Admission, scholarship tenure and assistantship responsibilities are separate records; satisfactory progress and the conditions of the issued award remain important after enrolment.

Build a personal cash-flow record for arrival, housing and ongoing costs using current quotes, the actual payment start and confirmed coverage. A monthly stipend is a resource to compare with those costs, not proof that every applicant’s budget will balance. Keep the signed award and renewal conditions with the admission decision, and ask the school about an unresolved term before relying on it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there one NUS Research Scholarship deadline?+

The central page directs January and August intake applicants to their faculty’s application deadlines. Use the intended programme’s current instructions.

Is the scholarship a blanket coursework master’s award?+

No. The identified qualification types arePhD andmaster’s by research; check the actual programme type.

Does admission automatically prove scholarship funding?+

No. Indicate scholarship interest in GradApp, complete the required funding steps and wait for the actual award decision.

Does every NUS research master’s programme have scholarship places?+

No. Check the school: Public Health currently says research MSc students must self-finance. The central scholarship degree list is not a promise of school-level availability.
