Erasmus Mundus GLOCAL: track, funding route and a complete application

Start with one suitable study track and the correct funding form. This guide turns GLOCAL’s current owner instructions into a practical track comparison and an original document-dependency plan.

Updated 16 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

The published GLOCAL 2027/29 scholarship deadline is 14 January 2027 at 13:00 UK time. Choose one active track and the scholarship form; later self-funded dates are different routes. Track H is not recruiting for this intake.

Match the funding form to the exact deadline

GLOCAL’s entry page separates the scholarship cutoff from its self-funded deadlines. In January, UK time is GMT, so the scholarship closing instant is also 13:00 UTC on 14 January 2027. For July and August, keep the stated UK local time rather than assume a year-round UTC offset.

The table is a route map, not an eligibility result. Check how the programme classifies your fee/application category; citizenship alone may not answer every fee-status question. A later self-funded date cannot rescue a scholarship file that missed the earlier required complete state.

Create one record containing cycle, track, funding form, category, deadline and the official page. If you later change funding route or track, inspect every field again instead of only replacing the date.

Choose an active track from the curriculum, not the destination photo

The application instructions say to select one track and identify H as not recruiting for 2027. The programme overview describes different partner sequences and academic emphases. For example, A concerns global history and creative industries, while B concerns global markets and development. These are curricular distinctions to investigate, not recommendations for a particular applicant.

Build the following original comparison before drafting a personal statement. Read modules and degree prerequisites, identify relevant prior study and note the gap you still need to resolve. An attractive mobility city is a preference; it does not establish academic preparation or a suitable thesis environment.

Fictional example: a business graduate has studied firms and trade but has not taken a formal history module. They compare the real course requirements and describe their actual preparation. They do not rename a marketing assignment as historical research simply to fit a track title.

Treat references as a dependency with a received state

The application page requires two references, with at least one academic. It specifies recent, signed institutional-headed/stamped evidence and gives institutional-email or applicant-upload routes with official contact details. Read its exact rules for when a professional reference is permitted; a personal email address or typed name is not a substitute.

Ask early and provide the referee with your genuine work, the selected track and the owner’s instructions. Keep requested, agreed, sent and received as different states. A lecturer’s promise to help is valuable, but it is not evidence that the correct reference has reached the permitted channel.

Original fictional dependency check: one referee has returned a compliant letter, while the second has only agreed by message. The applicant labels the second item pending and checks its accepted submission method. They do not upload their own summary of the relationship as though it were the referee’s endorsement.

Audit the academic and English evidence as separate items

The owner’s application list includes university-level transcripts and certificates, CV, identity and English evidence alongside the relevant funding form. Scholarship applicants are told to provide the required English evidence by the scholarship deadline. Use the programme’s current country/qualification rules instead of a generic GPA conversion or a universal MOI assumption.

Prepare a document register with original language, required translation, issue date and the exact upload state. Do not assume that an unconditional-offer condition can be left until enrolment if scholarship consideration requires it earlier.

Read the programme’s offer-status instructions carefully. The scholarship page describes an eligible offer state and excludes a file held for documents, while How to Apply describes admission offers following scholarship outcomes. If your status or sequence is unclear, seek clarification through the named programme/admissions contact rather than infer that a pending file qualifies. Retain the written response with its cycle and scope.

Write your own statement from an evidence bank

Use the statement to connect your actual preparation, the selected curriculum and a future learning or research question. This is an original planning method, not a statement to submit. The programme publishes its own prompt and word limit; read those before turning notes into an application answer.

For every claim, identify an example you could explain in an interview: the task, your own contribution, a result and what remains uncertain. Work experience is useful when connected to the programme’s learning goals, not merely listed as a sequence of impressive titles.

Fictional example: an applicant examined how a local firm changed suppliers after a market disruption. Their possible research question concerns how firms and local institutions respond to international pressures. They distinguish what they observed in that one assignment from a broad causal claim about all firms or countries. The application should remain in the applicant’s own words.

Plan mobility costs without adding the same scholarship money twice

GLOCAL’s scholarship page describes tuition coverage, insurance and two payment arrangements. One provides advance instalments deducted from the overall award with lower monthly payments; the other uses the stated full monthly allowance without those advance payments. Compare the actual offer and payment option, not the largest number from each alternative.

Original cash-timing exercise: an invented €600 deposit and €350 travel bill create €950 to arrange before arrival. If a verified advance will be received beforehand, record its date and amount once. If timing is uncertain, keep the pre-arrival gap unresolved instead of assuming a later monthly payment pays an earlier bill.

For a self-funded plan, replace scholarship expectations with verified personal funding and real quotations for every mobility location. An admission offer, scholarship shortlist or reserve position does not establish money available to spend.

Check the final file against the chosen route

Open the current track and funding instructions again before submission. Confirm that the selected track is recruiting, every required reference/document is in its permitted state and the final form uses the same programme choice as your statement.

After submission, retain the official confirmation and the exact final files privately. Follow the published programme channel for changes or follow-up. This worksheet helps organise a complete application; the consortium and university still assess academic eligibility, admission and funding.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a self-funded deadline for scholarship consideration?+

No. The published funding routes have separate deadlines. Use the scholarship row and its complete-file requirements.

Can I apply to Track H for 2027?+

The reviewed How to Apply page says Track H is not recruiting. Choose from the current active options and recheck before submission.

Does an agreed reference count as received?+

No. Track the reference through the permitted submission route and confirm the state required by the programme.

Does this guide settle the offer-status sequence?+

No. The owner pages describe the sequence differently. Ask the named programme/admissions contact about your actual status and retain the written clarification.
