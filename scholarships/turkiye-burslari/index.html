Türkiye Scholarships: eligibility and a stronger application

Türkiye Bursları combines financial support with university placement. Start by matching your degree level, age and academic preparation, then choose programmes whose teaching language and subject requirements you can actually meet.

Updated 24 Oct 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Türkiye Scholarships has different academic, age and programme conditions by level. Its annual full-time calendar is not the same as confirmation that the next call is open.

Next step: Check your route on the official criteria page, then shortlist programmes in TBBS when the relevant call is available.

Current status: prepare before the next full-time call

The official full-time programme page describes the recurring application period as 10 January–20 February. That calendar is useful for preparation; it is not evidence that a new application round is already open in October 2026. Confirm the named intake in TBBS before relying on a deadline.

Use the months before applications to obtain missing academic records, compare courses and ask suitable referees. A polished statement cannot repair an ineligible age, qualification or programme choice. Keep research and short-term schemes separate from the full-time degree route described here.

Check the level-specific rules first

The official criteria distinguish undergraduate and graduate applicants. The figures below are minimum screening conditions, not an indication that an applicant with that mark will be selected. Your qualification and the current call must also satisfy the published rules.

The official criteria exclude Turkish citizens and people who have lost Turkish citizenship, as well as applicants already enrolled at a Turkish university at the same level they intend to apply for. Graduating applicants should check the published completion timing, including the before-August condition.

Programme language is a separate decision

Do not read “without IELTS” as “no language evidence is ever required”. English-taught and other non-Turkish programmes can require an accepted language certificate, and some programmes require an admission test. Check the actual university requirements shown for the course.

Create a short programme sheet with teaching language, subject prerequisites, accepted certificates and your missing evidence. Keep the name of each qualification exactly as it appears on your transcript. A programme that looks attractive but does not match your preparation is a weak preference.

Understand placement, funding and your own budget

The full-time programme includes university/department placement, tuition, accommodation, health insurance, a Turkish-language year and specified flight support. Use the current award terms for allowance amounts and conditions. Do not substitute a social-media stipend figure for your award letter.

Separate covered benefits from costs you may incur before selection: obtaining documents, translations, tests, passport arrangements or travel to an interview. These are planning categories, not a claim that every applicant must pay each item. Do not spend on an agent who promises a scholarship place.

Build a document file that matches your chosen route

Use one folder per application with an evidence index. Mark an item as ready only after checking legibility, correct name, full pages and the format requested by the portal. When your degree is not yet complete, establish whether the call accepts a provisional certificate and by which date.

Documents such as international examination results, language certificates and a research proposal are conditional on level or programme. Do not upload an invented proposal or irrelevant test just to make a checklist longer.

Use TBBS as a sequence of decisions

Apply yourself through the official scholarship system. Draft long answers locally so that you can check spelling and preserve a copy; then follow the current field limits in the portal. A draft saved on your computer is not a submitted application.

  1. 1Create or access your own TBBS account on the official domain.
  2. 2Complete identity, education and other requested profile fields accurately.
  3. 3Compare the eligible programmes actually available to your profile.
  4. 4Enter preferences and explain the academic reason behind them.
  5. 5Upload the required evidence, review every section and submit when the call is open.
  6. 6Save the confirmation and monitor the official account for further instructions.

A letter of intent should explain a real academic choice

A useful answer connects previous preparation, the intended course and a credible next step. Avoid generic praise of Türkiye that would work for every subject. Explain one relevant project, what it taught you and which learning gap the chosen degree would address.

Teaching example

A programme-fit paragraph

In my final-year water-quality project, I compared low-cost monitoring methods and learned that our sampling design limited the conclusions. I now want structured training in environmental data analysis. When choosing programmes, I am comparing their methods modules and research opportunities rather than selecting by city alone.

Fictional teaching example. Use only your own project, verified programme information and the actual application prompt.

Review before you submit, not after the deadline

Read the complete application once as a reviewer who does not know you. Do the preferences agree with your subject background? Do dates, grades and activities match the evidence? Does each answer address the actual question?

For an interview invitation, use the official selection instructions to prepare. Retain your submitted answers so that you can discuss your choices honestly. The next useful action is to resolve the weakest required item, not to add more unrelated achievements.

Frequently asked questions

Can Pakistani students apply?+

Pakistani applicants should check the current eligible-applicant rules and the programmes available to their profile. Nationality alone does not establish eligibility or guarantee selection.

Is IELTS compulsory for every programme?+

No single rule covers every programme. A chosen teaching language or university may require an accepted certificate or another admission test.

Do I apply separately to a university?+

The full-time scholarship includes placement. Follow the scholarship portal instructions for your preferences rather than assuming a separate admission route applies.
