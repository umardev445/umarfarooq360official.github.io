Current application status and deadline

The official pages publish 20 October 2026 at 16:00 BST as the central 2027–2028 closing time. The status panel compares that cut-off with today's date. Confirm the portal before acting and do not assume the national nominator uses the same deadline.

The Master's and eligible-country PhD schemes have different nationality lists, purposes and applicant conditions. Start with the exact route rather than a general Commonwealth label.

Master's and PhD routes are not interchangeable

The Master's scholarship supports eligible candidates who could not otherwise afford UK study and are positioned to contribute to sustainable development. The PhD route covered here is intended for candidates from eligible least-developed countries and vulnerable Commonwealth states and supports full-time doctoral study.

Do not assume that eligibility for the Master's route proves eligibility for the PhD route. Check the current country list, academic requirements, development theme and all scheme-specific conditions.

Understand the two-part application route

Candidates apply through the CSC's stated online application system and must also use an approved nominating body. The CSC does not accept a candidate submitted only as a direct independent application outside the required nomination structure.

A national nominator, university or other approved organisation can impose its own documents, tests or earlier deadline. Identify the correct nominator for your country and route before drafting the final form.

What the funding may cover

The official pages describe approved tuition, travel to and from the United Kingdom, a living allowance and other applicable support such as study travel or thesis-related help. Some family or disability support may apply under stated conditions. Read the current terms instead of assuming every personal cost is covered.

Build the development-impact case

Define a development problem you understand, support it with context and explain why the chosen course or research route provides a missing capability. Then describe achievable outcomes, beneficiaries and the role you can realistically take after the award.

Connect past evidence to future plans. A candidate who has already taken relevant action can make a more credible case than one relying only on ambitious language.

  • Problem and relevant evidence
  • Your previous contribution
  • Skill or research gap
  • Why the selected UK programme fits
  • Short-term application after return
  • Longer-term measurable direction

Advice for applicants from Pakistan

Pakistan appears on the current Master's eligible-country list. Applicants must still satisfy all criteria and follow the approved Pakistani nominator process. For PhD funding, check the exact current eligible-country list rather than assuming the Master's country list applies.

Application and document checklist

Use the official page and nominator instructions to create one combined checklist. Keep degree names, dates, employment and proposed programme information consistent across both systems.

  • Correct scholarship route selected
  • Country eligibility confirmed
  • Approved nominator and local deadline identified
  • CSC online application completed
  • Academic documents meet stated standards
  • References follow the required process
  • Development impact is specific and evidence-based
  • Submission confirmations retained

Frequently asked questions

When is the Commonwealth Scholarship 2027 deadline?+

The current official Master's and listed PhD pages give 20 October 2026 at 16:00 BST. Your nominating body may use an earlier deadline or additional process.

Can I apply directly to the CSC without a nominator?+

The current scheme requires an application through the stated CSC system and an approved nominating body. A central form alone does not replace nomination.

Is Pakistan eligible for Commonwealth Master's Scholarships?+

Pakistan is on the current eligible-country list for the Master's route. All personal eligibility and nominator requirements still apply.

Is the Commonwealth Scholarship fully funded?+

The official scheme describes tuition, travel, living support and other applicable allowances. Read the exact current benefits and conditions for your route and circumstances.

Do I need university admission separately?+

Follow the current CSC and nominator instructions for course choices and admission. Scholarship nomination does not remove university academic requirements.
