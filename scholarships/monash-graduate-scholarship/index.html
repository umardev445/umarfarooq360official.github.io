Monash Graduate Scholarship: stipend, tuition and the 2027 round

MGS is a research-degree living stipend. International applicants must check tuition coverage separately and use the correct Australian-campus round before preparing the combined course and scholarship application.

Updated 23 Nov 2026Official source record ↓

Published by AspireCompass Editorial Team · Editorial contact: Hafiz Umar Farooq

The short answer

Monash advertises an MGS stipend ofAUD 41,100 per year at the 2027 rate. International tuition scholarships can be packaged separately. The central international Round 1 deadline for 2027 has moved to 28 February; minimum entry requirements do not guarantee funding.

Next step: Confirm the faculty, supervisor process, Australian campus and applicant category, then check the current course and scholarship instructions together.

First establish what the award actually pays

The MGS owner page describes support for research master’s and research doctorate students at Monash campuses in Australia. It provides a living allowance and relocation support where eligible. Tuition scholarships for international students are a separate package question. The listed 2027 annual stipend isAUD 41,100; do not turn that headline into an individual award decision or a promise that every cost is covered.

Use the following original record before comparing an offer. Keep a tuition reduction in the fee column and a cash stipend in the living-cost column. Ask about the first payment as well as the annual amount. A package can be generous overall while leaving an arrival payment to make before cash is available.

Use the 2027 Round 1 change, then check your faculty

The central research-admissions page lists the international Round 1 period as 1 September–28 February and explicitly says the 2027 close moved forward from 31 March. Its dates apply to Australian campuses. Domestic rounds and the Malaysia or Indonesia directions are different routes. Do not copy the international close into every applicant’s calendar.

The central and Science pages also differ on a later Round 3 close. That is a reason to check the intended faculty’s current instructions and resolve the applicable earlier requirement; it is not permission to choose the most convenient date. Keep a saved copy of the faculty guidance with the central round record. This guide does not infer a precise closing hour or one universal Round 3 date.

Prepare a research case before opening myApp

Start with a question you can explain rather than a list of university rankings. Read a potential group’s recent work and identify the connection between its methods and your preparation. A fictional candidate studying water sensors might distinguish field-data quality from sensor design, then choose which problem the intended group is equipped to supervise.

Write a short fit record: research question, relevant earlier project, proposed evidence and missing training. Explain what you personally did in any group work. An honest methodological gap can become a reason for seeking training; inventing an advanced technique you have never used creates a claim you cannot defend. Follow the chosen faculty’s supervisor and proposal process, since the central page says expectations vary.

Keep admission and scholarship completion in one task list

The owner directs applicants to use myApp for course admission and scholarship applications; eligible applicants are considered for available graduate research scholarships rather than choosing each specific award. Already-enrolled students should follow their applicable process. An admission-only pathway is not evidence that a late scholarship request will be considered.

Build your checklist from the current course and faculty instructions. Keep academic records with their grading explanation, language evidence, proposal, CV and referee tasks in a controlled folder. Where certification or translation is required, verify the permitted method before paying for it. For each upload, retain the final filename and purpose; avoid sending a mix of draft and final versions that describe different projects.

Original example: compare an admission offer and a funding package

A fictional international applicant receives admission and sees an MGS result pending. They should not record the place as funded. Their first budget has tuition and living costs unresolved. A second scenario can show what would change if both a stipend and an appropriate fee award were later confirmed, clearly marking that outcome conditional.

When decisions arrive, compare their periods and conditions. A stipend award does not by itself establish a tuition waiver; a fee award does not supply rent cash. If one part is missing, ask the university which costs remain and whether there is an applicable alternative. Keep speculative outside work or a future award out of the confirmed funding total.

Review the issued terms before accepting

Make a final record of the award name, covered period, payment timing and continuation terms. Read any restrictions on employment, overlapping awards, leave or changes to candidature in the applicable agreement. These are contract-specific questions; this guide does not invent a universal permission to work or pause the scholarship.

If you accept, keep the admission decision, each funding decision and the relevant conditions together. Revisit the budget if the start date, campus or study mode changes. A careful package review helps you distinguish an affordable research plan from a promising application that still has an unresolved cost.

Frequently asked questions

Does MGS automatically pay international tuition?+

MGS is a living stipend. The owner says international tuition scholarships can be packaged with it; require confirmation of the actual fee coverage.

What changed for the 2027 international Round 1 deadline?+

The central Monash page moved the close from 31 March to 28 February 2027. Check the intended faculty and Australian-campus instructions before using the date.

Can I apply through admission-only and assume scholarship consideration later?+

No. The owner requires scholarship applicants to use an open applicable scholarship round. Admission-only and scholarship consideration have different conditions.
