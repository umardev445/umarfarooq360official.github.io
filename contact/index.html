What to include

Send the listing title, the page URL, the specific correction and an official source that confirms the change. For new opportunities, include the programme owner's application page and current deadline.

  • Correction or submission type
  • AspireCompass page URL
  • Official programme URL
  • Exact detail to update
  • Your contact email

Editorial independence

Submission does not guarantee publication. We prioritise legitimate, clearly documented opportunities and may decline unclear, fee-based or unverifiable programmes.

What AspireCompass can and cannot answer

We can review reports about a page, broken official link or material programme change. We cannot make an official eligibility decision, review confidential application documents, contact a university on your behalf or predict a selection result.

Protect your personal information

Do not email passports, national identity cards, transcripts, bank records, account passwords, one-time codes or full application files. A correction normally needs only the public page URL, the exact issue and a supporting official source.

How submissions are reviewed

A suggestion is assessed for source quality, applicant value, transparency and safety. AspireCompass may edit a submitted description, wait for clearer official evidence or decline a programme. Submission does not create a commercial or advisory relationship.

Frequently asked questions

Can I submit my CV or scholarship essay for review?+

No. Please do not send personal application files. Use the public application guides and seek an appropriate adviser if you need individual support.

Can a programme owner request a correction?+

Yes. Include the exact AspireCompass URL, corrected detail and a current official source controlled by the programme owner.

Does sending an opportunity guarantee publication?+

No. Every suggestion is subject to independent editorial review, and unclear, unsafe or insufficiently documented programmes may be declined.
